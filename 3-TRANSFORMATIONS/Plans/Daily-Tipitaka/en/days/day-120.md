# Day 120 — What Is Here, What Is Not

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Yesterday ended mid-stream, watching the mind. Verse 366 closes that part with definitions. What does "the world" mean here? Just that mind, and the five heaps we cling to. What is covetousness? What is displeasure? The text says, then says they are set down.

Then the fourth foundation opens, and it has a shape the other three did not. Watching *dhammā* — the things that pass through the mind — is done as a roll-call of presence and absence. Is sense desire here right now? Say so. Is it not here? Say that too. Then: how does it come, how does it go, and how does it stay gone. Five hindrances first. Then the same roll-call with the seven awakening factors — mindfulness, investigation, energy, rapture, tranquillity, concentration, equanimity — asking not only whether each is here, but how it fills out.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What is actually in my mind right now — and can I just say so?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### BOJJHAṄGA — Awakening Factor

**Pronunciation:** *boj-jhaṅ-ga* — three syllables. Say "BOJ-jhang-ga"; the "jh" is a soft "j".

**In today's passage:** *Bojjhaṅga* joins *bodhi* (awakening) with *aṅga*, a limb or part. Seven are named: mindfulness, investigation, energy, rapture, tranquillity, concentration and equanimity. Today's verses put the same two questions to each one. Is it here? Is it not here? Then: what makes it arise, and what makes it grow full.

**Why it matters:** The hindrances are what you set down; the awakening factors are what you feed. Both lists work the same way — notice first, then act. Nothing on either list has to be invented. Each of the seven is already coming and going in you today, and naming one is most of the work of feeding it.
