# Day 111 — Both Ways

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The fourth and last angle: mutuality. Until now the chain has run one way — this, then that. Today it runs both. *With ignorance as condition, a formation; and with the formation as condition, ignorance too.*

Inside a single moment there is no earlier and later, so the leaning goes each way. The links hold each other up. But the text does not claim it everywhere. Clinging and becoming do not get the treatment, and neither does the sixth sense-field with mentality and material form — because in one moment those pairs do not in fact support each other both ways. The teachers are careful here. Take only what the moment really shows, and say nothing where it does not.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Is this feeding that — or are they feeding each other?*

**v. 275.** Therein, what is ignorance? The lack of knowledge, lack of vision… the barrier of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root — this is called "ignorance". *(…)* Therein, what is the mentality that has contact as condition too? The aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the mentality that has contact as condition too"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering".

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### AÑÑAMAÑÑA — Mutual

**Pronunciation:** *añ-ña-mañ-ña* — four syllables. The "ñ" is like the "ny" in "canyon."

**In today's passage:** *Aññamañña* means mutual — each one supporting the other. Today it is the last of the four angles on one moment of mind. Ignorance conditions the intention, and the intention conditions the ignorance right back.

**Why it matters:** Most of us look for the one cause and try to remove it. This says some things hold each other up instead, in a loop, with no first link to pull. Loops are not hopeless. But you undo them by weakening the whole circle, not by hunting for the start.
