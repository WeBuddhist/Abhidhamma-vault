# Day 127 — What the Measureless Asks For

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Chapter 10 finishes today, and with it the run of four short chapters that began with the foundations of mindfulness. The Abhidhamma section asks the seven awakening factors together and then one by one; the question section, from verse 482, puts them to the standard tests.

The answer that repeats is the one about object. What do the awakening factors take as their object? The measureless. Not a body, not a feeling, not a state of mind — the unbounded, which the texts name *nibbāna*. And because that object sits outside the five heaps a person is made of, the factors that take it are called outward-facing. The teaching sets this against the chapter's opening, where the first pass was ordinary and the middle two were mixed. Same seven factors throughout. What changed is the object.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What is my practice actually pointed at today?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### APPAMĀṆA — Measureless

**Pronunciation:** *ap-pa-mā-ṇa* — four syllables. Say "ap-pa-MAA-na".

**In today's passage:** *Appamāṇa* is what has no *pamāṇa* — no measure, no boundary, nothing to size it against. Today's verses use it twice of the awakening factors: they are measureless, and what they take as their object is measureless. Both point at the one thing, which the texts name *nibbāna*.

**Why it matters:** Everything else you have watched for a hundred and twenty-seven days has edges. A body ends, a feeling passes, a state of mind gives way. This word marks the single object that does not. You do not have to reach it today for it to be what you are facing.
