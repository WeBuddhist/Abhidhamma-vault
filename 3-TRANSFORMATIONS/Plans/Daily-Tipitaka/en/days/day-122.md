# Day 122 — Four Jobs, One Effort

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Chapter 8 is short, and it is all one move. Four right efforts: keep the harm that has not come from coming; let go of the harm that has; bring on the good that has not arrived; keep the good that has. Verse 390 states them, and each of the four is described in the same five steps — you generate aspiration, you strive, you initiate energy, you exert the mind, you endeavour.

Then the text slows down and defines those five, one at a time. The aspiration meant here is not craving. The teaching is careful about that: there is a wanting that comes from thirst, and a wanting that comes from wanting to do good, and only the second is in view. The verbs are graded too — you generate it, then keep generating it, then pick it up again when something knocks it down, then hold it steady.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Which of the four am I actually doing, and which am I only meaning to do?*

## 4. 💡 Reading for Meaning

**Verses 390–412.** *(Full text rendered in the WeBuddhist app from the verse library; the first and last verses of the range are printed below so the plan-importer can find the right entries.)*

**v. 390.** There are four right efforts. Here a monk generates aspiration, strives, initiates energy, exerts his mind, and endeavours for the non-arising of evil, unwholesome things that have not yet arisen; he generates aspiration, strives, initiates energy, exerts his mind, and endeavours for the abandoning of evil, unwholesome things that have arisen; he generates aspiration, strives, initiates energy, exerts his mind, and endeavours for the arising of wholesome things that have not yet arisen; he generates aspiration, strives, initiates energy, exerts his mind, and endeavours for the persistence of wholesome things that have arisen, for their non-confusion, their increase, their expansion, their development, their perfection.

…

**v. 412.** "Initiates energy": therein, what is energy? The mental initiation of energy… right effort, the awakening factor of energy, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "energy". He initiates this energy, undertakes it, practises it, develops it, cultivates it. Therefore it is said, "initiates energy".

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### SAMMAPPADHĀNA — Right Effort

**Pronunciation:** *sam-map-pa-dhā-na* — five syllables. Say "sam-MAP-pa-dhaa-na".

**In today's passage:** *Sammappadhāna* joins *sammā* (right, complete) with *padhāna*, exertion. Four are named and together they cover the whole field: two about harm, two about good; two about what has not happened yet, two about what has. They are called right because they are exertion with a reason, exertion with a method, exertion put where it works.

**Why it matters:** The four are a checklist you can run in under a minute. Most of us are strong at one and blind to another — quick to stop harm, careless about keeping good going. Naming all four is what shows you the one you have been skipping.
