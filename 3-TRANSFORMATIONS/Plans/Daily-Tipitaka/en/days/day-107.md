# Day 107 — Four Ways to Look

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Yesterday the sutta method finished and the Abhidhamma method began. Its move is to take the same twelve links and set them inside one moment of mind. Yesterday's verse gave the first of four angles: condition. Today the other three arrive. Cause — the link that stays with its result until the result breaks up. Association — the links that rise and fall together. Mutuality — where two links lean on each other, both ways.

Then comes the *mātikā*, the matrix: a bare list of the other eight starting points. Ignorance was the first. Formations, consciousness, mentality, the sixth sense-field, contact, feeling, craving, clinging — each can head its own run. Nine starting points, four angles each. Then the analysis proper opens, with an unwholesome mind.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Same moment — can I look at it more than one way?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### MĀTIKĀ — Matrix

**Pronunciation:** *mā-ti-kā* — three syllables. Say "MAA-ti-kaa."

**In today's passage:** A *mātikā* is a matrix — a bare list of headings that a longer teaching then fills in. Today's is short: eight more starting points for the same chain. The teachers count what it opens up. Nine starting points, four angles each — thirty-six sets, and a hundred and forty-four turns in all.

**Why it matters:** A matrix is a promise that nothing has been left out. You do not have to hold all hundred and forty-four. But it is worth knowing they are there. Whatever link you are standing on today, the teaching has a run that starts from it.
