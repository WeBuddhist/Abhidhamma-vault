# Day 121 — One Mindfulness, Four Names

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The chapter now says the same thing a third time, from further in. The discourse section spread the four foundations across many mind-moments — one to watch the body, another for feelings, another for the mind. Today's section, from verse 374, folds them into one.

The setting is a *lokuttara* mind — supramundane, above the world — on the path, leading out, leading to dismantling. In that single moment there is no queue of four practices. There is one mindfulness doing four jobs at once, and it takes four names for that reason. The closing verses then put the whole thing to the standard questions — wholesome or not, which feeling goes with it, what it takes as its object. The answer to the last one never changes. It is the unbounded, and the unbounded is *nibbāna*.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What would it be like for attention to be undivided, even once?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### LOKUTTARA — Supramundane

**Pronunciation:** *lo-kut-ta-ra* — four syllables. Say "lo-KUT-ta-ra".

**In today's passage:** *Lokuttara* joins *loka* (world) with *uttara*, going beyond — so, above the world. It marks the mind at the moment the path opens. Today's verses attach it to all four foundations at once: the body watched, the feelings watched, the mind watched, and what passes through the mind — all inside one mind that is leading out.

**Why it matters:** The word is not a claim about somewhere else. *Loka* was defined for you a few verses back: this body, these feelings, this mind, and what passes through it. Going beyond it is not leaving it. It is the same watching you did yesterday, kept up until it stops feeding what it watches.
