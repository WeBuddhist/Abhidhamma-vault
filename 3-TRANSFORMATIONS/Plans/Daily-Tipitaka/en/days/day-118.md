# Day 118 — Why We Practise At All

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The chapter closes. Today finishes the section where ignorance conditions the wholesome, then adds one more pass: results conditioned by a wholesome root, and results conditioned by an unwholesome root.

These last sections work by a different kind of conditioning, and the teachers are careful about it. Ignorance does not sit inside a wholesome moment. A wholesome root does not sit inside its own result, which may come much later. Both work as *upanissaya* — decisive support, the strong reason standing behind a thing rather than beside it. And notice what is left out. The functional minds get no chain here at all. Nothing supports them this way, because they are not going anywhere. With that, the analysis of dependent origination is finished.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What is standing behind today — not beside it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### UPANISSAYA — Decisive Support

**Pronunciation:** *u-pa-nis-sa-ya* — five syllables. Say "u-pa-NIS-sa-ya."

**In today's passage:** *Upanissaya* is decisive support — a condition that works from a distance rather than from alongside. Ignorance supports the wholesome in this way: it is why the path gets developed at all. A wholesome root supports its own result the same way, sometimes across a whole life.

**Why it matters:** Not every cause is in the room. Some of what shapes today was set going long ago and is still pulling. That cuts both ways. Old harm still reaches forward — and so does old good.
