# Day 106 — The Twelve Links

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

A new chapter opens — the sixth, and the longest in the book. It maps how one thing gives rise to the next. Ignorance, then formations. Formations, then consciousness. Then mentality and material form (mind and body), the six sense-fields, contact, feeling, craving, clinging, becoming, birth, and last of all aging and death, with sorrow and grief. Twelve links. Today the chain is stated, then each link is defined one at a time.

The teachers call this one of the four hardest things to teach. The Buddha taught it four ways — from the start, from the middle forward, from the end backward, from the middle back. Like four men gathering a creeper: one finds the root first, one the middle, one the tip. Why does ignorance come first? Not because it is a first cause. It has its own cause. It comes first because it is where the story of the round begins.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What is the link I keep landing on?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### PACCAYA — Condition

**Pronunciation:** *pac-ca-ya* — three syllables. The "c" is like the "ch" in "church."

**In today's passage:** *Paccaya* is a condition. The teachers put it plainly: what a result comes on account of, that is its condition. And the sense of the word is help — a condition is whatever helps a thing arise. This whole chapter is one long account of what helps what.

**Why it matters:** Nothing on the list stands alone. Not ignorance, not craving, not the mood you woke up in. Each one leans on something before it. That is not bad news. It means each one can be reached: change what helps a thing arise, and the thing changes.
