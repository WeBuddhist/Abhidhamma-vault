# Day 114 — What Stands Where Craving Stood

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Today is the wholesome section in full: the sense-sphere wholesome minds, one after another, each run through the chain.

Two swaps are worth watching. Where the unwholesome chain had craving, this one has *pasāda* — confidence, the mind settling into its object. Where it had clinging, this one has *adhimokkha* — decision, the mind coming down off the fence. The teachers say why the swap is needed at all: in one moment of a wholesome mind, craving and clinging are simply not there. Something has to fill those two places, and what fills them is the nearest thing of the same strength. Craving grips an object; confidence settles into one. Clinging holds fast; decision settles the matter. The shape is the same. The flavour is not.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Am I gripping this, or settling into it?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### PASĀDA — Confidence

**Pronunciation:** *pa-sā-da* — three syllables. Say "pa-SAA-da."

**In today's passage:** *Pasāda* is confidence — the mind going clear and settling into what it is on. Today it stands in the place craving holds inside an unwholesome mind. Both are the mind leaning in. One grabs; the other clears and rests.

**Why it matters:** A wholesome mind is not a flat mind. It leans in too. It just leans differently. Next time you notice yourself pulled toward something, the question is not whether to lean. It is which of these two is doing the leaning.
