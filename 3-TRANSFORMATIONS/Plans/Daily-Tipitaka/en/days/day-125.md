# Day 125 — No One Leads Twice

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Chapter 9 closes today. The Abhidhamma section runs the same four footings again at the path-moment, giving the longest stretch to the fourth — investigation — before the question section takes over at verse 462.

There the teaching makes a point that quietly limits the whole chapter. Ask which of the four governs at the path-moment, and the answer is: none of them. Four governing factors cannot govern each other. The image given is four princes of the same birth, the same age, the same strength, the same training — each senior in his own right, so not one of them bows. Whichever you led with, it led. The other three were there and working, not ranked below it.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Can I let the one that works lead, without making it the only one I trust?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### VĪMAṂSĀ — Investigation

**Pronunciation:** *vī-maṃ-sā* — three syllables. Say "VEE-mang-saa".

**In today's passage:** *Vīmaṃsā* is the mind turning something over to find out how it works. It is the fourth footing, and today's verses give it the most room — verses 454 to 461 belong to it alone. Where aspiration wants and energy pushes, investigation asks.

**Why it matters:** This is the footing for anyone who cannot make themselves want a thing they have not yet understood. The teaching does not treat that as the weaker start; Mogharāja reached the end this way. If your practice only moves once you can see why, that is a footing, not a fault.
