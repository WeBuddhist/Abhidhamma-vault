# Day 110 — Rising Together

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The third angle: association. Again the same moment, again the same links — and again one word added. *With ignorance as condition, a formation associated with ignorance.*

To be *sampayutta*, associated with, is a close thing. The teachers set four tests: the two arise together, cease together, rest on the same base, and take the same object. Not one after the other. Together. And notice a gap. Where the chain reaches mentality and material form, the word is dropped for the material side. Mental things and material things cannot be associated in this way — matter does not arise and cease with mind on those terms. So the text takes only what the word will honestly carry, and leaves the rest alone.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What arrived alongside this feeling, at the same instant?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### SAMPAYUTTA — Associated With

**Pronunciation:** *sam-pa-yut-ta* — four syllables. Say "sam-pa-YUT-ta."

**In today's passage:** *Sampayutta* means associated with. It is not loose company. Four things have to hold: the two arise together, cease together, share one base, and take one object. Today it is what joins ignorance to the intention inside a single moment.

**Why it matters:** It is easy to think a state of mind arrives alone and its companions turn up later. This says otherwise. The ignorance and the intention it drives come as one package, at one instant. Which means catching either one is catching both.
