# Day 116 — Results, Good and Bad

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

The indeterminate section continues. More results of wholesome action: mind-consciousness arising with joy, then with equanimity. Then the path minds — one developing the fine-material *jhāna* (meditative absorption), one the immaterial, one the path above the world. Then the mood turns. Results of unwholesome action: eye-consciousness again, the mind element again, but this time the object is unwished-for.

The chain does not change to suit us. It runs the same way for the pleasant result and for the unpleasant one. Same links, same positions, same shape. Only the action behind it differs. The teachers keep the machinery identical on purpose. A hard moment and an easy one are not two different kinds of thing. They are the same kind of thing with different histories.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Same machinery, different history — can I hold this that lightly?*

**v. 317.** Which things are indeterminate? On the occasion when, because sense-sphere wholesome action has been done and accumulated, the resultant mind-consciousness element has arisen, accompanied by equanimity, with a sight as its object, or a sound as its object, or a smell as its object, or a taste as its object, or a tangible thing as its object, or a mental object as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion, with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering.

**v. 327.** Therein, what is the formation? The intention, intending, state of intending — this is called the formation. *(…)* Therein, what is the existence that has decision as condition? Setting aside decision: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has decision as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering".

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### VIPĀKA — Result

**Pronunciation:** *vi-pā-ka* — three syllables. Say "vi-PAA-ka."

**In today's passage:** *Vipāka* is a result — a state of mind that arrives because of action already done and stored up. Today's verses run the chain through results of wholesome action, then through results of unwholesome action, with the links in exactly the same places.

**Why it matters:** A result is finished business. It arrived; it is not being chosen now. What is being chosen is the next thing. Keeping those two apart is most of the work. It takes some sting out of the arriving, and puts attention where it still counts.
