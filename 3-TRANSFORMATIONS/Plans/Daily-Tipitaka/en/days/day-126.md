# Day 126 — Seven, And When

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Chapter 10 opens with seven awakening factors. Verse 466 names them: mindfulness, investigation of things, energy, rapture, tranquillity, concentration, equanimity. The verses after take the seven through three passes. First, one mind-moment at a time. Then split into inner and outer. Then as things you grow on purpose.

Why seven, and not more or fewer? The teaching gives a plain answer. Three of them lift a mind gone slack: investigation, energy, rapture. Three settle a mind gone restless: tranquillity, concentration, equanimity. That leaves mindfulness. It is wanted in both cases, and in every other one. The text likens it to salt, which belongs in every dish.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *Is my mind slack right now, or restless? The answer picks the next three.*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### DHAMMAVICAYA — Investigation of Things

**Pronunciation:** *dham-ma-vi-ca-ya* — five syllables. Say "dham-ma-VI-cha-ya".

**In today's passage:** *Dhammavicaya* joins *dhamma* — a phenomenon, whatever the mind has in front of it — with *vicaya*, sorting. It is the second awakening factor. Today's verses define it simply. You take what you just noticed and ask of it: does this last? Does it satisfy? Is it mine?

**Why it matters:** Mindfulness notices. This one looks. That is the whole difference, and it is why the two come in that order. Noticing without looking leaves you well informed about a mind you still read wrong.
