# Day 113 — Where the Root Goes

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Yesterday the analysis started over for a second unwholesome mind. Today it finishes that sweep — the remaining unwholesome minds, each given the same treatment — and then turns a corner. Verse 292 asks a new question: *which things are wholesome?*

The turn matters. In an unwholesome mind, ignorance sat at the head of the chain. A wholesome mind has no ignorance in it, not in the same moment. So what stands in that place? The wholesome roots: non-greed, non-hate, non-delusion. The chain does not stop for a good mind. It runs the same shape, from a different root. The teachers note a second difference too. The unwholesome section put its matrix first and its analysis after. The wholesome section cannot, because the closing formula changes depending on whether the mind is worldly or above the world. So each kind gets its own short heading first.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *My better moments — what is at the root of those?*

**v. 281.** Therein, what is ignorance? The lack of knowledge, lack of vision… the barrier of ignorance, delusion, the unwholesome root — this is called "ignorance". *(…)* Therein, what is the existence that has decision as condition? Setting aside decision: the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, the aggregate of consciousness — this is called "the existence that has decision as condition"… therefore it is said, "thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering".

…

**v. 292.** Which things are wholesome? On the occasion when a wholesome mind of the sense sphere has arisen, accompanied by joy, associated with knowledge, with a sight as its object, or a sound as its object, or a smell as its object, or a taste as its object, or a tangible thing as its object, or a mental object as its object, or based on whatever it may be — on that occasion, with a wholesome root as condition, a formation; with the formation as condition, consciousness; with consciousness as condition, mentality; with mentality as condition, the sixth sense-field; with the sixth sense-field as condition, contact; with contact as condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, confidence; with confidence as condition, decision; with decision as condition, existence; with existence as condition, birth; with birth as condition, aging and death. Thus there is the origin of this entire mass of suffering.

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### KUSALAMŪLA — Wholesome Root

**Pronunciation:** *ku-sa-la-mū-la* — five syllables. Say "ku-sa-la-MOO-la."

**In today's passage:** A *kusalamūla* is a wholesome root. There are three: non-greed, non-hate, non-delusion. Today they take the place ignorance held all through the unwholesome section. Same chain, same twelve positions. A different thing at the head of it.

**Why it matters:** Good states are not just the absence of bad ones. They have roots of their own, and roots can be fed. Non-greed, non-hate and non-delusion are not blank spaces where greed, hate and delusion used to be. They are things you can grow.
