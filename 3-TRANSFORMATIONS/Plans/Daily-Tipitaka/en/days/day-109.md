# Day 109 — What Stays

## 1. 🧭 Today's Chanting Guide

Today the second angle: cause. The chain runs again, but a word is added to every link. Not just *with ignorance as condition, a formation* — but *a formation with ignorance as its root cause*.

The teachers explain the difference. The first phrase says ignorance helped. The second says something stronger: ignorance stays with the formation, right through, until the formation breaks up. It does not help and then leave. And notice where the added word stops. Becoming, birth, aging and death do not get it. Why not? Because clinging does not stay with becoming in that way, and birth does not stay with aging. Those links hand over and step back. The earlier ones do not. They are still there, holding the thing up, for as long as it lasts.

Today's question to carry into the chant: *What is still holding this up, right now?*

## 5. 🔑 Pāli Word of the Day

### HETU — Root Cause

**Pronunciation:** *he-tu* — two syllables. Say "HAY-too."

**In today's passage:** *Hetu* is a root cause. Today it marks the links that do not let go. A condition can help once and be finished with. A root cause stays with what it produced, all the way to the end of it.

**Why it matters:** This is worth testing against your own day. Some of what shaped your mood is over — it helped and left. Other parts are still running underneath, still holding it up. The second kind is the kind you can do something about, because it is still here.
