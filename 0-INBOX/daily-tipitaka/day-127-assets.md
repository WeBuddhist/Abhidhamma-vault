Na maggārammaṇā, siyā maggahetukā, siyā maggādhipatino; siyā na vattabbā maggahetukātipi, maggādhipatinotipi. Siyā uppannā, siyā anuppannā, siyā uppādino. Siyā atītā, siyā anāgatā, siyā paccuppannā. ## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye sattapi bojjhaṅge ekato pucchitvā vissajjanassa ca pāṭiyekkaṃ pucchitvā vissajjanassa ca vasena dve nayā. Upekkhāsambojjhaṅganiddese upekkhanavasena upekkhā, upekkhanākāro upekkhanā, upekkhitabbayutte samappavatte dhamme ikkhati na codetīti upekkhā, bojjhaṅgabhāvappattiyā lokiyaupekkhanāya adhikā upekkhanā ajjhupekkhanā, abyāpārāpajjanena majjhattabhāvo majjhattatā – sā ca cittassa na sattassāti dassetuṃ "majjhattatā cittassā"ti vuttaṃ. Nayā panettha ekato pucchitvā vissajjane ekekamagge nayasahassanti cattāri nayasahassāni, pāṭiyekkaṃ pucchitvā vissajjane ekekabojjhaṅgavasena cattāri cattārīti satta catukkā aṭṭhavīsati; tāni purimehi catūhi saddhiṃ dvattiṃsa nayasahassāni kusalāneva. **Yasmā pana phalakkhaṇepi bojjhaṅgā labbhanti, tasmā vipākanayopi āraddho** – iminā sammappadhānavibhaṅgato ayaṃ viseso, vipāke ca kusalato tiguṇā nayā kātabbā.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake pāḷianusāreneva bojjhaṅgānaṃ kusalādibhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu pana sabbepete appamāṇaṃ nibbānaṃ ārabbha pavattanato appamāṇārammaṇā eva, na maggārammaṇā; sahajātahetuvasena panettha kusalā maggahetukā; vīriyaṃ vā vīmaṃsaṃ vā jeṭṭhakaṃ katvā maggabhāvanākāle maggādhipatino; chandacittajeṭṭhikāya maggabhāvanāya na vattabbā maggādhipatinoti, phalakālepi na vattabbā eva. Atītādīsu ekārammaṇabhāvenapi na vattabbā; nibbānassa pana bahiddhādhammattā bahiddhārammaṇā nāma honti. Evaṃ imasmiṃ pañhāpucchakepi nibbattitalokuttarāva bojjhaṅgā kathitā; sammāsambuddhena hi suttantabhājanīyasseva paṭhamanaye lokiyā, dutiyatatiyesu lokiyalokuttaramissakā bojjhaṅgā kathitā, abhidhammabhājanīyassa pana catūsupi nayesu imasmiñca pañhāpucchake lokuttarāyevāti evamayaṃ bojjhaṅgavibhaṅgopi teparivaṭṭaṃ nīharitvā bhājetvā dassito.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo "upekkhitabbayutte samappavatte dhamme ikkhati na codetī"ti vutto, majjhattatā ca cittassa, na sattassāti dassitā; phalakkhaṇepi ca bojjhaṅgā labbhantīti vipākanayopi āraddho. Tasmā yoginā "ahaṃ majjhatto, mama upekkhā"ti attagāhaṃ pahāya, samappavattesu dhammesu acodanaṃ abyāvaṭataṃ upasampādetvā, "cittasseva ayaṃ majjhattatā, na puggalassā"ti sallakkhento, paggahaniggahakāle katakicco sārathi viya samappavattesu assesu ajjhupekkhanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake bojjhaṅgā appamāṇaṃ nibbānaṃ ārabbha pavattanato appamāṇārammaṇā, bahiddhārammaṇā ca vuttā; suttantabhājanīyassa paṭhamanaye lokiyā, dutiyatatiyesu missakā, abhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakesu pana lokuttarāyeva. Tasmā yoginā "ye me idāni uppajjanti bojjhaṅgā, teyeva lokuttarā"ti abhimānaṃ pahāya, lokiyato missakato lokuttarato ca anukkamena bhāvanaṃ upasampādetvā, "appamāṇaṃ ārammaṇaṃ appamāṇova bojjhaṅgo karotī"ti sallakkhento, parittesu ārammaṇesu cittaṃ asaṇṭhapento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

##### The Pairs ^2-10-3-2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-485]]

The awakening factor of investigation of things is a root; six awakening factors are associated with roots. The awakening factor of investigation of things is both a root and has roots; six awakening factors are not describable as being both roots and having roots; they have roots but are not roots. The awakening factor of investigation of things is both a root and associated with roots; six awakening factors are not describable as being both roots and associated with roots; they are associated with roots but are not roots. Six awakening factors are not roots yet have roots; the awakening factor of investigation of things is not describable either as being a non-root that has roots or as being a non-root without roots. They have conditions. They are conditioned. They are invisible. They offer no resistance. They are immaterial. They are beyond the world. They can be known by one means; they cannot be known by another. They are not mental taints. They are without mental taints and dissociated from mental taints. They are not describable either as being mental taints and subject to mental taints, or as being subject to mental taints but not mental taints. Nor as being mental taints and associated with mental taints, or as being associated with mental taints but not mental taints. They are dissociated from mental taints. They are without mental taints. They are not fetters… they are not ties… they are not floods… they are not yokes… they are not hindrances… they are not wrong grasping… they have objects. They are not minds. They are mental factors. They are associated with mind. They are conjoined with mind. They are originated by mind. They arise together with mind. They follow along with mind. They are conjoined with and originated by mind. They are conjoined with, originated by, and arise together with mind. They are conjoined with, originated by, and follow along with mind. They are external. They are not derived. They are not taken up at birth. They are not clinging… they are not defilements… they are not to be abandoned by seeing. They are not to be abandoned by meditative development. Their causes are not to be abandoned by seeing. Their causes are not to be abandoned by meditative development. They may be with thought; they may be without thought. They may be with examination; they may be without examination.
The awakening factor of joy is without joy; six awakening factors may be with joy or may be without joy. The awakening factor of joy is not accompanied by joy; six awakening factors may be accompanied by joy or may be not accompanied by joy. The awakening factor of joy is accompanied by happiness; six awakening factors may be accompanied by happiness or may be not accompanied by happiness. The awakening factor of joy is not accompanied by equanimity; six awakening factors may be accompanied by equanimity or may be not accompanied by equanimity. They do not belong to the sense sphere. They do not belong to the fine-material sphere. They do not belong to the immaterial sphere. They are not included in any sphere. They may be leading out; they may be not leading out. They may be fixed; they may be unfixed. They are unsurpassed. They are free from conflict.
