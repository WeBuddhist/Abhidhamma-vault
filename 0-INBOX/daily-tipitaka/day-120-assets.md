##### Dhammānupassanāniddeso ^2-7-1-4-0

Kathañca bhikkhu ajjhattaṃ dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati? Idha bhikkhu santaṃ vā ajjhattaṃ kāmacchandaṃ "atthi me ajjhattaṃ kāmacchando"ti pajānāti, asantaṃ vā ajjhattaṃ kāmacchandaṃ "natthi me ajjhattaṃ kāmacchando"ti pajānāti, yathā ca anuppannassa kāmacchandassa uppādo hoti tañca pajānāti, yathā ca uppannassa kāmacchandassa pahānaṃ hoti tañca pajānāti, yathā ca pahīnassa kāmacchandassa āyatiṃ anuppādo hoti tañca pajānāti. Evaṃ bhikkhu ajjhattabahiddhā dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṃ. ^2-367

Anupassīti. Tattha katamā anupassanā? Yā paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi – ayaṃ vuccati anupassanā. Imāya anupassanāya upeto hoti samupeto upāgato samupāgato upapanno sampanno samannāgato. Tena vuccati "anupassī"ti. ^2-368

Viharatīti. Iriyati vattati pāleti yapeti yāpeti carati viharati. Tena vuccati "viharatī"ti. ^2-369

Satimāti. Tattha katamā sati? Yā sati anussati…pe… sammāsati – ayaṃ vuccati "sati". Imāya satiyā upeto hoti…pe… samannāgato. Tena vuccati "satimā"ti. ^2-372

Tena vuccati "vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassa"nti.

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

#### Cittānupassanāniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Cittānupassanāniddese sarāganti aṭṭhavidhaṃ lobhasahagataṃ, vītarāganti lokiyakusalābyākataṃ; yasmā panidaṃ sammasanaṃ, na dhammasamodhānaṃ, tasmā ettha ekapadepi lokuttaraṃ na labbhati. Sadosanti duvidhaṃ domanassasahagataṃ, vītadosanti lokiyakusalābyākataṃ. Samohanti vicikicchāsahagatañceva uddhaccasahagatañcāti duvidhaṃ; yasmā pana moho sabbākusalesu uppajjati, tasmā imasmiṃyeva duke dvādasākusalacittāni pariyādiṇṇāni. Saṃkhittanti thinamiddhānupatitaṃ saṅkuṭitacittaṃ, vikkhittanti uddhaccasahagataṃ pasaṭacittaṃ. Mahaggatanti rūpāvacaraṃ arūpāvacarañca, amahaggatanti kāmāvacaraṃ; sauttaranti kāmāvacaraṃ, anuttaranti rūpārūpāvacaraṃ, tatrāpi sauttaraṃ rūpāvacaraṃ, anuttaraṃ arūpāvacarameva. Samāhitanti yassa appanāsamādhi upacārasamādhi vā atthi, asamāhitanti ubhayasamādhivirahitaṃ. Vimuttanti tadaṅgavikkhambhanavimuttīhi vinimuttaṃ; samucchedapaṭippassaddhinissaraṇavimuttīnaṃ pana idha okāsova natthi. Imasmimpi pabbe suddhavipassanāva kathitā.

#### Dhammānupassanāniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Yasmā kāyānupassanāya rūpakkhandhapariggaho, vedanānupassanāya vedanākkhandhapariggaho, cittānupassanāya viññāṇakkhandhapariggahova kathito, tasmā idāni sampayuttadhammasīsena saññāsaṅkhārakkhandhapariggahampi kathetuṃ dhammānupassanā dassitā. Nīvaraṇapabbe santaṃ abhiṇhasamudācāravasena, asantaṃ asamudācāravasena vā pahīnattā vā avijjamānaṃ; tattha subhanimitte ayonisomanasikārena kāmacchandassa, paṭighanimitte byāpādassa, aratitandivijambhikābhattasammadacetasolīnattesu thinamiddhassa, cetaso avūpasame uddhaccakukkuccassa, vicikicchāṭhānīyesu dhammesu vicikicchāya uppādo; asubhanimitte pana, mettāya cetovimuttiyā, ārambhanikkamaparakkamadhātūsu, cetaso vūpasame, kusalākusalādidhammesu ca yonisomanasikārena tesaṃ pahānaṃ. Cha cha dhammā ca ekamekassa pahānāya saṃvattanti, sabbattha kalyāṇamittatā sappāyakathā ca antogadhā; āyatiṃ anuppādo pana kāmacchandathinamiddhauddhaccānaṃ arahattamaggena, byāpādakukkuccānaṃ anāgāmimaggena, vicikicchāya sotāpattimaggena hoti. Bojjhaṅgapabbe santaṃ paṭilābhavasena, asantaṃ appaṭilābhavasena; satisambojjhaṅgassa cattāro, dhammavicayassa satta, vīriyassa ekādasa, pītiyā ekādasa, passaddhiyā satta, samādhissa ekādasa, upekkhāya pañca dhammā uppādāya saṃvattanti, sabbesañca bhāvanāpāripūri arahattamaggena hoti; visesato cettha vatthuvisadakiriyā indriyasamattapaṭipādanā ca vuttā – saddhāpaññānaṃ samādhivīriyānañca samatā pasatthā, sati pana loṇadhūpanaṃ viya sabbabyañjanesu sabbattha icchitabbā. Imesu dvīsu pabbesu suddhavipassanāva kathitā. Ime pana cattāro satipaṭṭhānā pubbabhāge nānācittesu labbhanti, lokuttaramaggakkhaṇe pana ekāva maggasampayuttā sati catukiccasādhanaṭṭhena cattāri nāmāni labhatīti ekacitteyeva labbhanti.

## Practice notes (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-practice.md`)

#### Cittānupassanāniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Cittānupassanāniddese sarāgādivasena soḷasavidhaṃ cittaṃ vibhattaṃ; ettha ca sammasanabhāvato lokuttaraṃ na labbhati, samucchedapaṭippassaddhinissaraṇavimuttīnañca okāso natthi. Tasmā yoginā "mama cittaṃ, ahaṃ cintemī"ti attagāhaṃ pahāya, sarāgaṃ vā cittaṃ "sarāga"nti sadosaṃ "sadosa"nti samohaṃ "samoha"nti saṅkuṭitaṃ "saṃkhitta"nti pasaṭaṃ "vikkhitta"nti yathābhūtaṃ jānanaṃ upasampādetvā, tadaṅgavikkhambhanavimuttivasena vimuttaṃ avimuttañca vavatthapento, uppannaṃ uppannaṃ cittaṃ appaṭicchādento anugacchanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Dhammānupassanāniddeso

[[#^toc-7-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Dhammānupassanāya sampayuttadhammasīsena saññāsaṅkhārakkhandhapariggaho kathito; nīvaraṇapabbe pañcannaṃ nīvaraṇānaṃ āhāro anāhāro ca, bojjhaṅgapabbe sattannaṃ bojjhaṅgānaṃ uppādakāraṇāni ca dassitāni. Tasmā yoginā subhanimitte paṭighanimitte aratiādīsu cetaso avūpasame vicikicchāṭhānīyesu ca dhammesu ayonisomanasikāraṃ pahāya, asubhanimittassa uggahaṃ mettāya cetovimuttiṃ ārambhanikkamaparakkamadhātuyo cetaso vūpasamaṃ kusalākusaladhammavavatthānañca yonisomanasikārena upasampādetvā, indriyesu guttadvāratāya bhojane mattaññutāya kalyāṇamittatāya sappāyakathāya ca nīvaraṇāni vinodetvā, satisampajaññena satisambojjhaṅgaṃ, paripucchakatāya vatthuvisadakiriyāya indriyasamattapaṭipādanāya ca dhammavicayaṃ, apāyabhayaānisaṃsapaccavekkhaṇehi vīriyaṃ, buddhādianussatīhi pītiṃ, sappāyabhojanautuiriyāpathasevanāya passaddhiṃ, nimittakusalatāya samaye paggahaniggahasampahaṃsanajjhupekkhaṇāhi samādhiṃ, sattasaṅkhāramajjhattatāya upekkhaṃ samuṭṭhāpento – sabbattha ca satiṃ loṇadhūpanaṃ viya icchamāno – cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

"Contemplating"… "dwells"… "ardent"… "clearly comprehending"… "mindful"… "having removed covetousness and grief with regard to the world": therein, what is the world? That very mind is the world; the five aggregates that can be clung to are also the world. Therein, what is covetousness? The lust, passion… passion of the mind — this is called "covetousness". Therein, what is grief? Mental discomfort, mental pain, the uncomfortable painful experience born of mind-contact, the uncomfortable painful feeling born of mind-contact — this is called "grief". So this covetousness and this grief are removed in this world, thoroughly removed, stilled, calmed, quieted, gone to rest, completely gone to rest, extinguished, fully extinguished, dried up, completely dried up, brought to an end. Therefore it is said, "having removed covetousness and grief with regard to the world".
The Exposition of Contemplating the Mind. ^2-366

##### The Exposition of Contemplating Mental Objects ^2-7-1-4-0

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-367]]

And how does a monk dwell contemplating mental objects in mental objects internally? Here a monk, when sense desire is present in him, knows "sense desire is present in me"; when sense desire is not present in him, he knows "sense desire is not present in me"; he knows how unarisen sense desire arises; he knows how arisen sense desire is abandoned; and he knows how abandoned sense desire does not arise again in the future. When ill will is present in him… when dullness and drowsiness are present in him… when restlessness and remorse are present in him… when doubt is present in him, he knows "doubt is present in me"; when doubt is not present in him, he knows "doubt is not present in me"; he knows how unarisen doubt arises; he knows how arisen doubt is abandoned; and he knows how abandoned doubt does not arise again in the future.
When the awakening factor of mindfulness is present in him, he knows "the awakening factor of mindfulness is present in me"; when the awakening factor of mindfulness is not present in him, he knows "the awakening factor of mindfulness is not present in me"; he knows how the unarisen awakening factor of mindfulness arises; and he knows how the arisen awakening factor of mindfulness is perfected by development. When the awakening factor of investigation of things is present in him… when the awakening factor of energy is present in him… when the awakening factor of joy is present in him… when the awakening factor of tranquillity is present in him… when the awakening factor of concentration is present in him… when the awakening factor of equanimity is present in him, he knows "the awakening factor of equanimity is present in me"; when the awakening factor of equanimity is not present in him, he knows "the awakening factor of equanimity is not present in me"; he knows how the unarisen awakening factor of equanimity arises; and he knows how the arisen awakening factor of equanimity is perfected by development. He practises that sign, develops it, cultivates it, and establishes it well. Having practised that sign, developed it, cultivated it, and established it well, he directs his mind to mental objects externally.

When the awakening factor of investigation of things is present in him… when the awakening factor of energy is present in him… when the awakening factor of joy is present in him… when the awakening factor of tranquillity is present in him… when the awakening factor of concentration is present in him… when the awakening factor of equanimity is present in him, he knows "the awakening factor of equanimity is present in him"; when the awakening factor of equanimity is not present in him, he knows "the awakening factor of equanimity is not present in him"; he knows how the unarisen awakening factor of equanimity arises; and he knows how the arisen awakening factor of equanimity is perfected by development. "Contemplating": therein, what is contemplation? The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view — this is called contemplation. He is endowed with this contemplation, fully endowed, having reached it, fully reached it, possessed of it, complete in it, furnished with it. Therefore it is said, "contemplating". ^2-368

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-369]]

"Dwells": he moves, goes on, keeps going, maintains himself, carries on, conducts himself, dwells. Therefore it is said, "dwells". ^2-369

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-370]]

"Ardent": therein, what is ardour? The mental initiation of energy… right effort — this is called "ardour". He is endowed with this ardour… furnished with it. Therefore it is said, "ardent". ^2-370

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-371]]

"Clearly comprehending": therein, what is clear comprehension? The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view — this is called "clear comprehension". He is endowed with this clear comprehension… furnished with it. Therefore it is said, "clearly comprehending". ^2-371

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-372]]

"Mindful": therein, what is mindfulness? The mindfulness, recollection… right mindfulness — this is called "mindfulness". He is endowed with this mindfulness… furnished with it. Therefore it is said, "mindful". ^2-372
