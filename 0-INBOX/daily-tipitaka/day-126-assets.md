#### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ ^2-10-1-0

Satta bojjhaṅgā – satisambojjhaṅgo, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pītisambojjhaṅgo, passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, samādhisambojjhaṅgo, upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo. ^2-466

Tattha katamo satisambojjhaṅgo? Idha bhikkhu satimā hoti paramena satinepakkena samannāgato, cirakatampi cirabhāsitampi saritā hoti anussaritā – ayaṃ vuccati "satisambojjhaṅgo".
So tathā sato viharanto taṃ dhammaṃ paññāya pavicinati pavicarati parivīmaṃsamāpajjati – ayaṃ vuccati "dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo".
Tassa taṃ dhammaṃ paññāya pavicinato pavicarato parivīmaṃsamāpajjato āraddhaṃ hoti vīriyaṃ asallīnaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "vīriyasambojjhaṅgo".
Āraddhavīriyassa uppajjati pīti nirāmisā – ayaṃ vuccati "pītisambojjhaṅgo".
Pītimanassa kāyopi passambhati, cittampi passambhati – ayaṃ vuccati "passaddhisambojjhaṅgo".
Passaddhakāyassa sukhino cittaṃ samādhiyati – ayaṃ vuccati "samādhisambojjhaṅgo".
So tathā samāhitaṃ cittaṃ sādhukaṃ ajjhupekkhitā hoti – ayaṃ vuccati "upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo". ^2-467

Satta bojjhaṅgā – satisambojjhaṅgo, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pītisambojjhaṅgo, passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, samādhisambojjhaṅgo, upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo. ^2-468

Tattha katamo satisambojjhaṅgo? Atthi ajjhattaṃ dhammesu sati, atthi bahiddhā dhammesu sati. Yadapi ajjhattaṃ dhammesu sati tadapi satisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati. Yadapi bahiddhā dhammesu sati tadapi satisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati.
Tattha katamo dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo? Atthi ajjhattaṃ dhammesu pavicayo, atthi bahiddhā dhammesu pavicayo. Yadapi ajjhattaṃ dhammesu pavicayo tadapi dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati. Yadapi bahiddhā dhammesu pavicayo tadapi dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati.
Tattha katamo vīriyasambojjhaṅgo? Atthi kāyikaṃ vīriyaṃ, atthi cetasikaṃ vīriyaṃ. Yadapi kāyikaṃ vīriyaṃ tadapi vīriyasambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati. Yadapi cetasikaṃ vīriyaṃ tadapi vīriyasambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati.
Tattha katamo pītisambojjhaṅgo? Atthi savitakkasavicārā pīti, atthi avitakkaavicārā pīti. Yadapi savitakkasavicārā pīti tadapi pītisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati. Yadapi avitakkaavicārā pīti tadapi pītisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati.
Tattha katamo passaddhisambojjhaṅgo? Atthi kāyapassaddhi [kāyappassaddhi (syā. ka.)], atthi cittapassaddhi [cittappassaddhi (syā. ka.)]. Yadapi kāyapassaddhi tadapi passaddhisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati. Yadapi cittapassaddhi tadapi passaddhisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati.
Tattha katamo samādhisambojjhaṅgo? Atthi savitakko savicāro samādhi, atthi avitakko avicāro samādhi. Yadapi savitakko savicāro samādhi tadapi samādhisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati. Yadapi avitakko avicāro samādhi tadapi samādhisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati.
Tattha katamo upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo? Atthi ajjhattaṃ dhammesu upekkhā, atthi bahiddhā dhammesu upekkhā. Yadapi ajjhattaṃ dhammesu upekkhā tadapi upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati. Yadapi bahiddhā dhammesu upekkhā tadapi upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṃvattati. ^2-469

Satta bojjhaṅgā – satisambojjhaṅgo, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pītisambojjhaṅgo, passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, samādhisambojjhaṅgo, upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo. ^2-470

Tattha katamo satisambojjhaṅgo? Idha bhikkhu satisambojjhaṅgaṃ bhāveti vivekanissitaṃ virāganissitaṃ nirodhanissitaṃ vossaggapariṇāmiṃ, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgaṃ bhāveti…pe… vīriyasambojjhaṅgaṃ bhāveti… pītisambojjhaṅgaṃ bhāveti… passaddhisambojjhaṅgaṃ bhāveti… samādhisambojjhaṅgaṃ bhāveti… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgaṃ bhāveti vivekanissitaṃ virāganissitaṃ nirodhanissitaṃ vossaggapariṇāmiṃ.
Suttantabhājanīyaṃ. ^2-471

#### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ ^2-10-2-0

Satta bojjhaṅgā – satisambojjhaṅgo, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pītisambojjhaṅgo, passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, samādhisambojjhaṅgo, upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo. ^2-472

Tattha katame satta bojjhaṅgā? Idha bhikkhu yasmiṃ samaye lokuttaraṃ jhānaṃ bhāveti niyyānikaṃ apacayagāmiṃ diṭṭhigatānaṃ pahānāya paṭhamāya bhūmiyā pattiyā vivicceva kāmehi…pe… paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ upasampajja viharati dukkhapaṭipadaṃ dandhābhiññaṃ, tasmiṃ samaye satta bojjhaṅgā honti – satisambojjhaṅgo…pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo. ^2-473

Tattha katamo satisambojjhaṅgo? Yā sati anussati…pe… sammāsati satisambojjhaṅgo maggaṅgaṃ maggapariyāpannaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "satisambojjhaṅgo".
Tattha katamo dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo? Yā paññā pajānanā…pe… amoho dhammavicayo sammādiṭṭhi dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo maggaṅgaṃ maggapariyāpannaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo".
Tattha katamo vīriyasambojjhaṅgo? Yo cetasiko vīriyārambho…pe… sammāvāyāmo vīriyasambojjhaṅgo maggaṅgaṃ maggapariyāpannaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "vīriyasambojjhaṅgo".
Tattha katamo pītisambojjhaṅgo? Yā pīti pāmojjaṃ āmodanā pamodanā hāso pahāso vitti odagyaṃ [odaggaṃ (sī.) dha. sa. 9] attamanatā cittassa pītisambojjhaṅgo – ayaṃ vuccati "pītisambojjhaṅgo".
Tattha katamo passaddhisambojjhaṅgo? Yā vedanākkhandhassa saññākkhandhassa saṅkhārakkhandhassa viññāṇakkhandhassa passaddhi paṭippassaddhi passambhanā paṭippassambhanā paṭippassambhitattaṃ passaddhisambojjhaṅgo – ayaṃ vuccati "passaddhisambojjhaṅgo".
Tattha katamo samādhisambojjhaṅgo? Yā cittassa ṭhiti…pe… sammāsamādhi samādhisambojjhaṅgo maggaṅgaṃ maggapariyāpannaṃ – ayaṃ vuccati "samādhisambojjhaṅgo".
Tattha katamo upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo? Yā upekkhā upekkhanā ajjhupekkhanā majjhattatā cittassa upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo – ayaṃ vuccati "upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo". Ime vuccanti satta bojjhaṅgā. Avasesā dhammā sattahi bojjhaṅgehi sampayuttā. ^2-474

## Section summary (from `2-RAILS/Sections/pi-2-summaries.md`)

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-1|↑↑↑]]

Lakkhaṇato upaṭṭhānalakkhaṇo satisambojjhaṅgo, pavicayalakkhaṇo dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, paggahalakkhaṇo vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pharaṇalakkhaṇo pītisambojjhaṅgo, upasamalakkhaṇo passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, avikkhepalakkhaṇo samādhisambojjhaṅgo, paṭisaṅkhānalakkhaṇo upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo; sabbesaṃ upakārakattā sati paṭhamaṃ vuttā. Tayo cettha nayā. Paṭhamanaye satisambojjhaṅganiddese paññā saṅgahitā satiyā balavabhāvadīpanatthaṃ – yathā rājaputtaṃ gahetvā ṭhito mahāmatto attanopi rājaputtassapi tejena tejavā hoti, evaṃ paññāya saddhiṃ uppannā sati balavatī, vinā uppannā dubbalā; iti apubbaṃ acarimaṃ ekacittakkhaṇe nānārasalakkhaṇā pubbabhāgavipassanābojjhaṅgā kathitā. Dutiyanaye ajjhattabahiddhābhedena satipavicayā, kāyikacetasikabhedena vīriyaṃ, kāyacittabhedena passaddhīti satta cuddasa honti; porāṇakattherā cettha ajjhattadhammesu satipavicayaupekkhā attano khandhārammaṇattā lokiyā, appattamaggaṃ kāyikavīriyampi lokiyaṃ, avitakkaavicārā pana pītisamādhiyo kāmarūpārūpāvacarato nikkhantattā nibbattitalokuttarāva, sesā missakāti vibhajitvā dassesuṃ. Tatiyanaye bhāvanāvasena vivekanissitaṃ virāganissitaṃ nirodhanissitaṃ vossaggapariṇāmiñca bhāveti – viveko ca pañcavidho tadaṅgavikkhambhanasamucchedapaṭippassaddhinissaraṇavasena, vossaggo ca duvidho pariccāgavossaggo pakkhandanavossaggoti; vipassanākkhaṇe hi tadaṅgavasena maggakkhaṇe ca samucchedavasena kilese pariccajati, tanninnabhāvena ca ārammaṇakaraṇena ca nibbānaṃ pakkhandati.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye sattapi bojjhaṅge ekato pucchitvā vissajjanassa ca pāṭiyekkaṃ pucchitvā vissajjanassa ca vasena dve nayā. Upekkhāsambojjhaṅganiddese upekkhanavasena upekkhā, upekkhanākāro upekkhanā, upekkhitabbayutte samappavatte dhamme ikkhati na codetīti upekkhā, bojjhaṅgabhāvappattiyā lokiyaupekkhanāya adhikā upekkhanā ajjhupekkhanā, abyāpārāpajjanena majjhattabhāvo majjhattatā – sā ca cittassa na sattassāti dassetuṃ "majjhattatā cittassā"ti vuttaṃ. Nayā panettha ekato pucchitvā vissajjane ekekamagge nayasahassanti cattāri nayasahassāni, pāṭiyekkaṃ pucchitvā vissajjane ekekabojjhaṅgavasena cattāri cattārīti satta catukkā aṭṭhavīsati; tāni purimehi catūhi saddhiṃ dvattiṃsa nayasahassāni kusalāneva. **Yasmā pana phalakkhaṇepi bojjhaṅgā labbhanti, tasmā vipākanayopi āraddho** – iminā sammappadhānavibhaṅgato ayaṃ viseso, vipāke ca kusalato tiguṇā nayā kātabbā.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo "upekkhitabbayutte samappavatte dhamme ikkhati na codetī"ti vutto, majjhattatā ca cittassa, na sattassāti dassitā; phalakkhaṇepi ca bojjhaṅgā labbhantīti vipākanayopi āraddho. Tasmā yoginā "ahaṃ majjhatto, mama upekkhā"ti attagāhaṃ pahāya, samappavattesu dhammesu acodanaṃ abyāvaṭataṃ upasampādetvā, "cittasseva ayaṃ majjhattatā, na puggalassā"ti sallakkhento, paggahaniggahakāle katakicco sārathi viya samappavattesu assesu ajjhupekkhanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

There are seven awakening factors: the awakening factor of mindfulness, the awakening factor of investigation of things, the awakening factor of energy, the awakening factor of joy, the awakening factor of tranquillity, the awakening factor of concentration, the awakening factor of equanimity. ^2-466

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-467]]

Therein, what is the awakening factor of mindfulness? Here a monk is mindful, endowed with the highest mindfulness and care; he remembers and recalls what was done long ago and said long ago — this is called "the awakening factor of mindfulness".
Dwelling thus mindful, he investigates that teaching with wisdom, examines it, and enters into a full inquiry of it — this is called "the awakening factor of investigation of things".
As he investigates that teaching with wisdom, examines it, and enters into a full inquiry of it, energy is initiated in him without slackening — this is called "the awakening factor of energy".
In one whose energy is initiated, unworldly joy arises — this is called "the awakening factor of joy".
In one whose mind is joyful, the body becomes tranquil and the mind becomes tranquil — this is called "the awakening factor of tranquillity".
In one whose body is tranquil and who is happy, the mind becomes concentrated — this is called "the awakening factor of concentration".
He is one who watches well over the mind thus concentrated, with equanimity — this is called "the awakening factor of equanimity". ^2-467

Therein, what are the seven awakening factors? Here a monk, on the occasion when he develops the meditative absorption that is beyond the world, leading out, leading to dismantling, for the abandoning of views, for reaching the first stage — secluded from sense pleasures… he enters and dwells in the first meditative absorption, of difficult practice and slow direct knowledge — on that occasion there are seven awakening factors: the awakening factor of mindfulness… the awakening factor of equanimity. ^2-473

![[1-SOURCES/Text/pi-2.md#^2-474]]

Therein, what is the awakening factor of mindfulness? The mindfulness, recollection… right mindfulness, the awakening factor of mindfulness, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "the awakening factor of mindfulness".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of investigation of things? The wisdom, understanding… non-delusion, investigation of things, right view, the awakening factor of investigation of things, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "the awakening factor of investigation of things".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of energy? The mental initiation of energy… right effort, the awakening factor of energy, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "the awakening factor of energy".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of joy? The joy, gladness, rejoicing, delight, mirth, merriment, felicity, elation, satisfaction of the mind, the awakening factor of joy — this is called "the awakening factor of joy".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of tranquillity? The tranquillity of the aggregate of feeling, the aggregate of perception, the aggregate of mental formations, and the aggregate of consciousness, their calming down, becoming tranquil, calming, state of being calmed, the awakening factor of tranquillity — this is called "the awakening factor of tranquillity".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of concentration? The steadiness of the mind… right concentration, the awakening factor of concentration, a path factor, included in the path — this is called "the awakening factor of concentration".
Therein, what is the awakening factor of equanimity? The equanimity, being equanimous, watching over with equanimity, neutrality of the mind, the awakening factor of equanimity — this is called "the awakening factor of equanimity". These are called the seven awakening factors. The remaining things are associated with the seven awakening factors. ^2-474
