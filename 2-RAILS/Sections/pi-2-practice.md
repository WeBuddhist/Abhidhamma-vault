## Khandhavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-1|↑↑↑]]

Khandhavibhaṅge pañca khandhā rāsilakkhaṇena koṭṭhāsalakkhaṇena ca vibhattā. Yassa yogino "rūpaṃ ruppanaṭṭhena rūpaṃ, vedanā vedayitalakkhaṇā, saññā sañjānanalakkhaṇā, saṅkhārā āyūhanalakkhaṇā, viññāṇaṃ vijānanalakkhaṇa"nti pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ sabhāvo pākaṭo hoti, so "na ettha koci satto na puggalo, rāsimattameva ida"nti passanto attasaññaṃ pajahati. Tasmā yoginā pañcasu khandhesu "netaṃ mama, nesohamasmi, na meso attā"ti anupassitvā, khandhesu attagāhaṃ akaraṇena pāpaṃ pahāya, aniccadukkhānattalakkhaṇāropanena vipassanaṃ upasampādetvā, cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ. Ayañhi khandhavibhaṅgo attadiṭṭhisamugghātanatthaṃ desito.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-5]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-7]]

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ

[[#^toc-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye pañca khandhā ekādasasu okāsesu — atītānāgatapaccuppanna-ajjhattabahiddhā-oḷārikasukhuma-hīnapaṇīta-dūresantikavasena — vibhattā. Ayaṃ ekādasavidho vibhāgo yogino sabbapariyādānatthaṃ upakappati: yaṃ kiñci rūpaṃ vā vedanā vā, atītaṃ vā anāgataṃ vā, attano vā parassa vā, oḷārikaṃ vā sukhumaṃ vā, sabbaṃ tadekajjhaṃ abhisaññūhitvā "khandho"ti passati. Tasmā yoginā ekekasmiṃ okāse attasaññaṃ apanetvā, "sabbaṃ idaṃ khandhamattaṃ aniccaṃ vipariṇāmadhamma"nti sammasitvā, kāmarāgaṃ paṭighañca tesu ṭhānesu pahāya, sabbaso pariggahaṃ paṭṭhapetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-6]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-7]]

#### Rūpakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-1|↑↑↑]]

Rūpakkhandhaniddese "ruppanaṭṭhena rūpa"nti vuttaṃ — sītuṇhādivasena vikāraṃ āpajjanato rūpaṃ nāma. Atītaṃ rūpaṃ niruddhaṃ vigataṃ vipariṇataṃ atthaṅgataṃ, anāgataṃ ajātaṃ anuppannaṃ, paccuppannaṃ jātaṃ uppannaṃ samuṭṭhitaṃ. Evaṃ ruppanasabhāvaṃ passanto yogī sarīre "subha"nti saññaṃ pajahati. Tasmā yoginā attano parassa ca kāye cattāro mahābhūte upādārūpañca "idaṃ ruppati vipariṇamatī"ti pariggaṇhitvā, kāyasmiṃ rāgaṃ mānañca pahāya, asubhānupassanaṃ aniccānupassanañca upasampādetvā, kāye anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-8]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-9]]

#### Vedanākkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Vedanākkhandhaniddese sukhā dukkhā adukkhamasukhā ca vedanā kāyikacetasikavasena vibhattā; tāsaṃ atītādibhāvo santativasena khaṇādivasena ca veditabbo. Oḷārikasukhumaniddese akusalā vedanā sadarathaṭṭhena dukkhavipākaṭṭhena ca oḷārikā, kusalā niddarathaṭṭhena sukhavipākaṭṭhena ca sukhumā. Yathā ca vedanā khaṇattayapariyāpannā uppajjitvā nirujjhati, evaṃ passanto tattha nābhinandati nāppaṭikkosati. Tasmā yoginā sukhāya vedanāya rāgaṃ, dukkhāya paṭighaṃ, adukkhamasukhāya avijjañca pahāya, "vedanā aniccā saṅkhatā paṭiccasamuppannā"ti sallakkhetvā, vedanānupassanaṃ bhāvetvā, vedanāsu anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Saññākkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhaniddese cakkhusamphassajādivasena cha saññā vuttā — purimā pañca cakkhupasādādivatthukāva, manosamphassajā hadayavatthukāpi avatthukāpi. Paṭighasamphassajā nāma sappaṭighe vatthumhi sappaṭighe ārammaṇe ca uppannā. Saññā hi nimittaṃ gaṇhāti; tato ca papañco vaḍḍhati. Tasmā yoginā dvāresu ārammaṇaṃ āpāthagataṃ "saññāmattaṃ ida"nti ñatvā, nimittaggāhena anusārena uppajjamānaṃ kāmasaññaṃ byāpādasaññaṃ vihiṃsāsaññañca pahāya, nekkhammasaññaṃ abyāpādasaññaṃ avihiṃsāsaññañca upasampādetvā, "aniccā saññā"ti anupassanena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-59]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-60]]

#### Saṅkhārakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-4|↑↑↑]]

Saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese "cetanā"ti heṭṭhimakoṭiyā padhānasaṅkhāravasena vuttaṃ — cetanā hi āyūhanaṭṭhena pākaṭattā padhānā, tāya gahitāya taṃsampayuttasaṅkhārā gahitāva honti. Cetanā ca kammaṃ nāma; tasmā ayaṃ khandho kammassa mūlaṃ dasseti. Tasmā yoginā "cetanāhaṃ, bhikkhave, kammaṃ vadāmī"ti ñatvā, akusalacetanāya kāyavacīmanoduccaritaṃ pahāya, kusalacetanāya dānasīlabhāvanākammaṃ upasampādetvā, sabbasaṅkhāre "aniccā vipariṇāmadhammā"ti passanto saṅkhāresu nibbindanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-64]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-65]]

#### Viññāṇakkhandho

[[#^toc-1-1-5|↑↑↑]]

Viññāṇakkhandhaniddese cakkhuviññāṇādīni pañca cakkhupasādādivatthukāneva, manoviññāṇaṃ hadayavatthukampi avatthukampi; sabbaṃ catubhūmakaṃ viññāṇaṃ. Na sakkā viññāṇaṃ "manosamphassaja"nti niddisituṃ — viññāṇameva hi mano nāma. Yassa yogino "viññāṇaṃ paccayuppannaṃ, cakkhuñca paṭicca rūpe ca uppajjati cakkhuviññāṇa"nti pākaṭaṃ hoti, so "attā viññātā"ti gāhaṃ pajahati. Tasmā yoginā viññāṇe attagāhaṃ pahāya, "paṭiccasamuppannaṃ ida"nti sallakkhetvā, cittānupassanaṃ bhāvetvā, viññāṇe anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-66]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-67]]

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ

[[#^toc-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye satthā khandhe bhājento tike gahetvā dukesu pakkhipi, duke gahetvā tikesu pakkhipi, ubhatovaḍḍhananīhārena āhari, avasāne bahuvidhena dassesi — iti cattāro mahāvārā. Etaṃ pana bahudhā desanaṃ puggalajjhāsayena ceva desanāvilāsena ca katanti veditabbaṃ: yassa yena ākārena paṭivijjhanaṃ sappāyaṃ, tassa tenākārena desesi. Tasmā yoginā "nāyaṃ dhammo ekeneva nayena gahetabbo"ti ñatvā, attano cittassa sappāyaṃ kammaṭṭhānanayaṃ gahetvā, mānaṃ diṭṭhiparāmāsañca pahāya, nānānayehi khandhe sammasanto paññaṃ upasampādetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-120]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-121]]

#### Rūpakkhandho (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye rūpakkhandhaniddeso dhammasaṅgaṇiyaṃ rūpakaṇḍe vitthāritanayeneva veditabbo — tattha ekavidhādivasena rūpassa vibhāgo vitthārito. Tasmā yoginā heṭṭhā rūpakaṇḍe pariggahitaṃ rūpassa ruppanasabhāvaṃ idhāpi anussaritvā, "sabbaṃ rūpaṃ cattāri mahābhūtāni upādārūpañcā"ti paricchinditvā, rūpe subhasaññaṃ niccasaññañca pahāya, kāye satiṃ upaṭṭhapetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-116]]

#### Vedanākkhandho (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye vedanākkhandho ekavidhena phassasampayuttato, duvidhena sahetukāhetukato, tividhena jātito, catubbidhena bhūmantarato, pañcavidhena indriyato, chabbidhena vatthuto, sattavidhādivasena ca bhedato dassito. Catubhūmikavedanā ca cakkhudvārādīsu tīhi ākārehi labbhati — upanissayavasena, samatikkamavasena, bhāvanāvasena. Tattha yathā ekacco jātijarābyādhimaraṇaṃ bhayato disvā "na sakkā nipajjitvā niddāyantena jātiādīhi muccituṃ, āditova vīriyaṃ kātuṃ sīlaṃ sodhetuṃ vaṭṭatī"ti cintetvā arahattaṃ pāpuṇāti, evaṃ yoginā saṃvegaṃ upanissayaṃ katvā sīlaṃ sodhetabbaṃ, uppanne kilese samatikkamitvā, nāmarūpaṃ pariggaṇhitvā tīṇi lakkhaṇāni āropetvā vipassanāpaṭipāṭiyā saṅkhāre sammasitvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Saññā-Saṅkhāra-Viññāṇakkhandhā (Abhidhamma)

[[#^toc-1-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Saññākkhandhādayo vedanākkhandhe vuttena upāyena veditabbā. Saññākkhandhaniddese tikesu vedanāttikapītittikāpi labbhanti; saṅkhārakkhandhaniddese phassassāpi saṅkhārakkhandhapariyāpannattā "cittasampayutto"ti vuttaṃ; viññāṇakkhandhaniddese "cakkhuviññāṇa"ntiādi vuttaṃ, na hi sakkā viññāṇaṃ manosamphassajanti niddisituṃ. Iti ekasmiṃ nayamhi gahite sesā tenupāyena gahitāva honti. Tasmā yoginā ekasmiṃ khandhe sammasanañāṇaṃ paṭiladdhaṃ sesesupi upanetvā, "sabbe pañca khandhā aniccā dukkhā anattā"ti ekarasena passanto, khandhesu attagāhaṃ pahāya, sabbaso anupādāya cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ

[[#^toc-1-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchane pañcannaṃ khandhānaṃ kusalākusalābyākatādivasena pañhā pucchitvā vissajjitā. Ārammaṇattikesu cattāro khandhā kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha rajjantassa dussantassa muyhantassa vā parittārammaṇā honti, saṃvarantassa sammasantassa vā tatheva; maggaphalanibbānāni paccavekkhantassa appamāṇārammaṇā. Attano khandhe ārabbha ajjhattārammaṇā, paresaṃ ārabbha bahiddhārammaṇā. Iti ekasmiṃyeva ārammaṇe rajjanaṃ vā saṃvaraṇaṃ vā hoti. Tasmā yoginā "ārammaṇe patiṭṭhitaṃ me cittaṃ rajjati vā saṃvarati vā"ti sallakkhetvā, ārammaṇesu rāgadosamohe pahāya, tesuyeva saṃvaraṃ sammasanañca upasampādetvā, ajjhattabahiddhā khandhe samaṃ passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## Āyatanavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-2|↑↑↑]]

Āyatanavibhaṅge dvādasāyatanāni ajjhattikabāhiravasena vibhattāni. Āyatanato, āyānaṃ tananato, āyatassa ca nayanato āyatanaṃ nāma: anamatagge saṃsāre pavattaṃ atīva āyataṃ saṃsāradukkhaṃ yāva na nivattati, tāva etāni nayanteva pavattayanti. Tāni ca ajjhattikāni suññagāmo viya daṭṭhabbāni dhuvasubhasukhattabhāvavirahitattā, bāhirāni gāmaghātakacorā viya ajjhattikānaṃ abhighātakattā; apica ajjhattikāni cha pāṇakā viya, bāhirāni tesaṃ gocarā viya. Imasmiṃ vibhaṅge ca tathāgatena dvādasannaṃ āyatanānaṃ anattalakkhaṇaṃ dassitaṃ. Tasmā yoginā chasu dvāresu asaṃvutacittatāya uppajjamānaṃ abhijjhādomanassaṃ pahāya, indriyasaṃvaraṃ satiñca upasampādetvā, "suñño gāmo eso, netaṃ mama, nesohamasmi, na meso attā"ti dvādasasu āyatanesu anattānupassanaṃ āropetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Āyatana)

[[#^toc-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye "cakkhu aniccaṃ dukkhaṃ anattā vipariṇāmadhamma"ntiādinā dvādasannaṃ āyatanānaṃ vipassitabbākāro dassito. Tattha hutvā abhāvaṭṭhena aniccaṃ, paṭipīḷanaṭṭhena dukkhaṃ, avasavattanaṭṭhena anattā; suññato assāmikato akāmakāriyato attapaṭikkhepatoti imehi catūhi kāraṇehipi anattā veditabbaṃ. Imāni pana lakkhaṇāni amanasikārā appaṭivedhā ca na upaṭṭhahanti: aniccalakkhaṇaṃ santatiyā paṭicchannattā, dukkhalakkhaṇaṃ iriyāpathehi paṭicchannattā, anattalakkhaṇaṃ ghanena paṭicchannattā. Tasmā yoginā udayabbayaṃ pariggahetvā santati vikopetabbā, abhiṇhasampaṭipīḷanaṃ manasikatvā iriyāpatho ugghāṭetabbo, nānādhātuyo vinibbhujitvā ghanavinibbhogo kātabbo; evaṃ āyatanesu niccasubhasukhattasaññaṃ pahāya, tīṇi lakkhaṇāni yāthāvasarasato upaṭṭhāpetvā, imaṃ sabbesu āyatanesu kathitaṃ sammasanacāraṃ paṭipajjantena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-165]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-169]]
[[1-SOURCES/Commentaries/pi-vibhanga-atthakatha.md#^2-171]]

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Āyatana)

[[#^toc-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye heṭṭhā vipassakānaṃ upakāratthāya yugalato vuttāni āyatanāni idha tathā avatvā ajjhattikabāhiravavatthānanayena vuttāni, ajjhattikabāhirānaṃ sabbākārato sabhāvadassanatthaṃ. Dhammāyatananiddese ca "asaṅkhatā dhātū"ti asaṅkhatasabhāvaṃ nibbānaṃ vuttaṃ; yasmā panetaṃ āgamma rāgādayo khīyanti, tasmā "rāgakkhayo dosakkhayo mohakkhayo"ti vuttaṃ, tīṇipi hetāni nibbānasseva adhivacanāni. Tasmā yoginā "ajjhattikañca bāhirañca sabbaṃ idaṃ sabhāvamattaṃ nissattaṃ"ti sabbākārato pariggaṇhitvā, dvāresu uppannaṃ rāgaṃ dosaṃ mohañca pahāya, tesaṃ khayāya nibbidāvirāganissayaṃ vipassanaṃ upasampādetvā, asaṅkhatāya amatāya dhātuyā adhimuccanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Āyatana)

[[#^toc-2-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake dasannaṃ āyatanānaṃ rūpabhāvena abyākatatā veditabbā, manāyatanadhammāyatanānaṃ pana khandhavibhaṅge catunnaṃ khandhānaṃ viya kusalākusalābyākatabhāvo. Tāni hi dve āyatanāni pañcapaṇṇāsa kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha rajjantassa dussantassa muyhantassa saṃvarantassa sammasantassa paccavekkhantassa ca parittārammaṇāni honti; attano āyatanāni ārabbha ajjhattārammaṇāni, paresaṃ āyatanāni ārabbha bahiddhārammaṇāni. Iti ekasmiṃyeva ārammaṇe rajjanaṃ vā saṃvaraṇaṃ vā hoti, kusalākusalatā ca dvīsuyeva āyatanesu ṭhitā, na rūpāyatanādīsu abyākatesu. Tasmā yoginā "na ārammaṇaṃ doso, cittameva rajjati dussati muyhati cā"ti sallakkhetvā, dvāresu rajjanaṃ dussanaṃ muyhanañca pahāya, tesuyeva ārammaṇesu saṃvaraṃ sammasanañca upasampādetvā, ajjhattabahiddhā āyatanāni samaṃ passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## Dhātuvibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-3|↑↑↑]]

Dhātuvibhaṅge dhātvaṭṭho nāma sabhāvaṭṭho, sabhāvaṭṭho nāma suññataṭṭho, suññataṭṭho nāma nissattaṭṭho; "dhātū"ti hi nijjīvamattassetaṃ adhivacanaṃ. Tenevāha bhagavā "cha dhāturo ayaṃ, bhikkhu, puriso"ti; jīvasaññāsamūhanatthañhi dhātudesanaṃ akāsi. Santi hi sattā vijānanasabhāve viññāṇe jīvasaññino; tesaṃ cakkhuviññāṇādibhedena tassā anekattaṃ, cakkhurūpādipaccayāyattavuttitāya aniccatañca pakāsetvā dīgharattānusayitaṃ jīvasaññaṃ samūhanitukāmena bhagavatā dhātuyo pakāsitā. Tasmā yoginā attano attabhāve "ahaṃ mamā"ti jīvasaññaṃ attagāhañca pahāya, "suññaṃ idaṃ nissattaṃ dhātumattaṃ paccayāyattavutti"nti dhātuvinibbhogaṃ upasampādetvā, ghanavinibbhoge kate anattalakkhaṇaṃ yāthāvasarasato upaṭṭhāpetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Dhātu)

[[#^toc-3-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye tīhi chakkehi dhātuyo saṅkhipitvā dassitā; tesu paṭhamena dhātumanasikāro dassito. Imasmiṃ pana dhātumanasikāre kammaṃ katvā vipassanaṃ paṭṭhapetvā uttamatthaṃ arahattaṃ pāpuṇitukāmena catupārisuddhisīlaṃ sodhetabbaṃ — sīlavato hi kammaṭṭhānabhāvanā ijjhati; visuddhasīlena ca sīle patiṭṭhāya dasa pubbapalibodhā chinditabbā, chinnapalibodhena sattavidhena uggahakosallena dasavidhena ca manasikārakosallena kammaṭṭhānaṃ nijjaṭaṃ paguṇaṃ kātabbaṃ, kesādīsu ekekakoṭṭhāso "acetano abyākato suñño nissatto"ti manasikātabbo. Tatiyacchakke ca kāmadhātu byāpādadhātu vihiṃsādhātūti tayo akusalavitakkā, nekkhammadhātu abyāpādadhātu avihiṃsādhātūti tayo kusalā dassitā; abyāpādo ca mettāya adhivacanaṃ, avihiṃsā karuṇāya, nekkhammaṃ sabbassāpi kusalassa. Byāpādo hi sattesu uppannoyeva kammapathaṃ bhindati, tathā vihiṃsā. Tasmā yoginā kāmabyāpādavihiṃsāvitakke pahāya, nekkhammaṃ mettaṃ karuṇañca upasampādetvā, sīle patiṭṭhitena dhātumanasikārena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Dhātu)

[[#^toc-3-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake aṭṭhārasannaṃ dhātūnaṃ heṭṭhā vuttanayānusāreneva kusalākusalābyākatabhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu ca pañcannaṃ cakkhuviññāṇādīnaṃ manodhātuyā ca ekantena pañcasu rūpārammaṇādīsu pavattito parittārammaṇatā; dhammadhātumanoviññāṇadhātūnaṃ pana manāyatanadhammāyatanesu vuttanayeneva parittamahaggatāppamāṇārammaṇatā veditabbā. Iti soḷasa dhātuyo kāmāvacarā, dve catubhūmikā lokiyalokuttaramissakā kathitā. Tasmā yoginā "pañcadvāre uppannā viññāṇadhātu parittārammaṇāva, manoviññāṇadhātu pana appamāṇārammaṇāpi hotī"ti sallakkhetvā, parittesu ārammaṇesu rajjanaṃ dussanaṃ muyhanañca pahāya, manoviññāṇadhātuṃ maggaphalanibbānesu upanento appamāṇārammaṇaṃ bhāvanaṃ upasampādetvā, kāmāvacarato uttari nīharanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## Saccavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-4|↑↑↑]]

Saccavibhaṅge cattāri ariyasaccāni anūnāni anadhikāni desitāni; pariññeyyapahātabbasacchikātabbabhāvetabbānaṃ vasena, taṇhāvatthutaṇhātaṇhānirodhataṇhānirodhupāyānaṃ vasena, ālayālayarāmatāālayasamugghātaālayasamugghātūpāyānañca vasena cattāreva vuttāni. Bhavasukhassādagadhitānaṃ sattānaṃ saṃvegajananatthaṃ paṭhamaṃ dukkhaṃ vuttaṃ, "neva taṃ akataṃ āgacchati, na issaranimmānādito hotī"ti ñāpanatthaṃ tadanantaraṃ samudayo, saṃviggamānasānaṃ dukkhanissaraṇagavesīnaṃ assāsajananatthaṃ nirodho, tadadhigamatthaṃ ante maggo. Rogo viya hi dukkhasaccaṃ, roganidānamiva samudayasaccaṃ, rogavūpasamo viya nirodhasaccaṃ, bhesajjamiva maggasaccaṃ. Tasmā yoginā dukkhaṃ pariññeyyanti pariggaṇhitvā, samudayaṃ pahātabbanti taṇhaṃ ālayañca pahāya, maggaṃ bhāvetabbanti sīlasamādhipaññākkhandhe upasampādetvā, nirodhaṃ sacchikātabbanti cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Sacca)

[[#^toc-4-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye cattāri saccāni vitthārena vibhattāni; tāni ca vitthārato āgataṭṭhāne vitthāreneva kathetabbāni. Tattha dukkhassa pīḷanaṭṭho saṅkhataṭṭho santāpaṭṭho vipariṇāmaṭṭho, samudayassa āyūhanaṭṭho nidānaṭṭho saṃyogaṭṭho palibodhaṭṭho, nirodhassa nissaraṇaṭṭho vivekaṭṭho asaṅkhataṭṭho amataṭṭho, maggassa niyyānaṭṭho hetvaṭṭho dassanaṭṭho ādhipateyyaṭṭhoti cattāro cattāro atthā tathā avitathā anaññathā abhisametabbā. Dukkhañāṇañhi pariyuṭṭhānābhibhavanavasena pavattamānaṃ sakkāyadiṭṭhiṃ nivatteti, samudayañāṇaṃ ucchedadiṭṭhiṃ, nirodhañāṇaṃ sassatadiṭṭhiṃ, maggañāṇaṃ akiriyadiṭṭhiṃ; tathā phale hetumhi nirodhe upāye ca vippaṭipattiṃ nivatteti. Yathā ca sattadhā bhinnassa vālassa koṭiyā koṭiṃ paṭivijjhanato duppaṭivijjhataraṃ etesaṃ saccānaṃ yathābhūtapaṭivedho, tathā appamādena ghaṭitabbaṃ. Tasmā yoginā catūsu saccesu sammohaṃ micchādiṭṭhiñca pahāya, uggahaparipucchāsavanadhāraṇasammasanavasena saccābhisamayaṃ upasampādetvā, "idaṃ dukkhaṃ, ayaṃ dukkhasamudayo, ayaṃ dukkhanirodho, ayaṃ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā"ti yathābhūtaṃ pajānanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Samudayasaccaṃ

[[#^toc-4-1-2|↑↑↑]]

Samudayasaccaniddese "yāyaṃ taṇhā ponobbhavikā nandirāgasahagatā tatratatrābhinandinī"ti vuttaṃ. Sā punabbhavaṃ dadamānāpi adadamānāpi punabbhavakaraṇasīlattā ponobbhavikā, abhinandanasaṅkhātena nandirāgena atthato ekattaṃ gatattā nandirāgasahagatā, yatra yatra attabhāvo rūpādīsu vā ārammaṇesu tatra tatra abhinandanato tatratatrābhinandinī; kāmataṇhā bhavataṇhā vibhavataṇhāti ca tividhā. Cakkhādīsu hi mamattena abhiniviṭṭhā sattā attano cakkhuṃ ugghāṭitamaṇisīhapañjaraṃ viya, sotaṃ rajatapanāḷikaṃ viya, ghānaṃ haritālavaṭṭiṃ viya, jivhaṃ rattakambalapaṭalaṃ viya, kāyaṃ suvaṇṇatoraṇaṃ viya, manaṃ aññesaṃ manena asadisaṃ uḷāraṃ maññanti; tesaṃ evaṃ maññamānānaṃ tāni cakkhādīni piyarūpāni ceva honti sātarūpāni ca, atha nesaṃ tattha anuppannā ceva taṇhā uppajjati, uppannā ca punappunaṃ nivisati. Tasmā yoginā piyarūpe sātarūpe mamattaṃ maññanañca pahāya, tesuyeva vatthūsu asubhasaññaṃ aniccasaññañca upasampādetvā, "ayaṃ taṇhā dukkhassa mūlaṃ, ayaṃ samudayo pahātabbo"ti sallakkhento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Nirodhasaccaṃ

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Sacca)

[[#^toc-4-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake catunnampi saccānaṃ khandhavibhaṅge vuttanayānusāreneva kusalākusalābyākatabhāvo veditabbo. Ārammaṇattikesu pana samudayasaccaṃ kāmāvacaradhamme assādentassa parittārammaṇaṃ hoti, mahaggatadhamme assādentassa mahaggatārammaṇaṃ, paññattiṃ assādentassa navattabbārammaṇaṃ; dukkhasaccaṃ kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha uppannaṃ parittārammaṇaṃ, nava lokuttaradhamme paccavekkhaṇakāle appamāṇārammaṇaṃ; maggasaccaṃ vīriyaṃ vā vīmaṃsaṃ vā jeṭṭhakaṃ katvā maggabhāvanākāle maggādhipati hoti. Iti dve saccāni lokiyāni, dve lokuttarāni. Tasmā yoginā "assādanavasena me cittaṃ samudayapakkhaṃ bhajati, paccavekkhaṇavasena maggapakkha"nti sallakkhetvā, parittesupi mahaggatesupi dhammesu assādanaṃ pahāya, vīriyavīmaṃsajeṭṭhakena maggabhāvanaṃ upasampādetvā, lokuttaradhamme paccavekkhanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## Indriyavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-5|↑↑↑]]

Indriyavibhaṅge bāvīsati indriyāni anūnāni anadhikāni desitāni. "Indaṭṭhaṃ kāretī"ti indriyaṃ — cakkhudvāre cakkhundriyaṃ, vijānanalakkhaṇe manindriyaṃ, anupālanalakkhaṇe jīvitindriyaṃ, adhimokkhalakkhaṇe saddhindriyaṃ, paggahalakkhaṇe vīriyindriyaṃ, upaṭṭhānalakkhaṇe satindriyaṃ, avikkhepalakkhaṇe samādhindriyaṃ, dassanalakkhaṇe paññindriyanti. Ādhipaccasaṅkhātena issariyaṭṭhenāpi etāni indriyāni; cakkhādīsu hi tikkhesu tikkhattā, mandesu mandattā, cakkhuviññāṇādidhammā attano ākāraṃ anuvattanti. Yathā ca indo attano vijite issariyaṃ kāreti, evaṃ ekamekaṃ indriyaṃ attano visaye. Tasmā yoginā "asāminikāni me indriyānī"ti maññanaṃ indriyesu asaṃvutabhāvañca pahāya, indriyasaṃvaraṃ saddhāvīriyasatisamādhipaññindriyānañca bhāvanaṃ upasampādetvā, "saṃvegabahulo bhikkhu, ṭhito indriyasaṃvare; indriyāni pariññāya, dukkhassantaṃ nigacchatī"ti vuttanayena cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Indriya)

[[#^toc-5-1|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye ekamekassa indriyassa kiccaṃ dassitaṃ. Cakkhundriyādīnaṃ tāva attano tikkhamandādibhāvena cakkhuviññāṇādidhammānaṃ attākārānuvattāpanaṃ kiccaṃ; manindriyassa sahajātadhammānaṃ attano vasavattāpanaṃ; jīvitindriyassa sahajātadhammānupālanaṃ; sukhadukkhasomanassadomanassindriyānaṃ sahajātadhamme abhibhavitvā yathāsakaṃ oḷārikākārānupāpanaṃ; upekkhindriyassa santapaṇītamajjhattākārānupāpanaṃ; saddhādīnaṃ paṭipakkhābhibhavanaṃ sampayuttadhammānañca pasannākārādibhāvasampāpanaṃ; anaññātaññassāmītindriyassa saṃyojanattayappahānaṃ; aññindriyassa kāmarāgabyāpādādītanukaraṇapahānaṃ; aññātāvindriyassa sabbakiccesu ussukkappahānanti. Desanākkamopi paṭipattiṃ dasseti — attabhāvapariyāpannehi cakkhādīhi ārabhitvā, "yaṃ kiñci vedayitaṃ sabbaṃ taṃ sukhadukkha"nti ñāpetvā, taṃnirodhatthaṃ bhāvetabbāti saddhādīni desitāni, ante ca paramassāsabhūtaṃ aññātāvindriyaṃ. Tasmā yoginā sukhadukkhasomanassadomanassindriyānaṃ oḷārikākārena vasavattitaṃ pahāya, saddhāvīriyasatisamādhipaññindriyānaṃ paṭipakkhābhibhavanakiccaṃ upasampādetvā, "imāya paṭipattiyā esa dhammo paṭhamaṃ attani pātubhavatī"ti passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Indriya)

[[#^toc-5-2|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake bāvīsatindriyānaṃ kusalākusalābyākatabhāvo ārammaṇādivibhāgo ca pāḷinayānusārena veditabbo. Tattha satta rūpindriyāni anārammaṇāni; sukhadukkhadvayaṃ ekantaparittārammaṇaṃ; domanassindriyaṃ kāmāvacaradhamme ārabbha parittārammaṇaṃ, mahaggate ārabbha mahaggatārammaṇaṃ, paññattiṃ ārabbha navattabbārammaṇaṃ; tīṇi lokuttarindriyāni bahiddhārammaṇāni. Bhūmito ca dasindriyāni kāmāvacarāni, tīṇi lokuttarāni, nava pana lokiyalokuttaramissakāni — manindriyajīvitindriyasomanassindriyaupekkhindriyāni ceva saddhāvīriyasatisamādhipaññindriyapañcakañca. Tasmā yoginā "aññāni me lokiyakāle indriyāni, aññāni lokuttarakāle"ti vikappaṃ pahāya, tesuyeva navasu missakindriyesu — visesato saddhādipañcake — nirantarabhāvanaṃ upasampādetvā, "yeheva ajja appamatto kusalaṃ upasampādemi, teheva parato dukkhassantaṃ nigacchāmī"ti sallakkhento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-6|↑↑↑]]

Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅge dvādasaṅgiko paccayākāro desito. "Saccaṃ satto paṭisandhi, paccayākārameva ca; duddasā caturo dhammā, desetuñca sudukkarā"ti hi porāṇā; tasmā sakasamayaṃ avokkamantena parasamayañca anāyūhantena ayamattho gahetabbo. Avijjādīheva kāraṇehi ayaṃ kevalo dukkhakkhandho samudeti, na issaranimmānādīhi, na ca akāraṇā. Yathā ca vallihārako valliyā mūlaṃ chinditvā sabbaṃ ākaḍḍhati, evaṃ yogināpi mūlato paṭṭhāya paccayākāro sallakkhetabbo. Duggatigāmino hi kammassa visesahetu avijjā, sugatigāmino bhavataṇhā. Tasmā yoginā "issaro vā kāraṇaṃ, ahetukaṃ vā ida"nti micchādiṭṭhiṃ "ahaṃ karomī"ti mānañca pahāya, hetuphalesu yathābhūtañāṇaṃ upasampādetvā, avijjāya ca bhavataṇhāya ca vaṭṭamūlabhāvaṃ passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭicca)

[[#^toc-6-1|↑↑↑]]

Suttantabhājanīye nānācittavasena paccayākāro vibhatto. Tattha kāyaduccaritādi avindiyaṃ vindatīti, kāyasucaritādi vindiyaṃ na vindatīti ca avijjā; saccānaṃ tathaṭṭhaṃ indriyānaṃ ādhipateyyaṭṭhañca aviditaṃ karotītipi avijjā. Aññāṇalakkhaṇā avijjā sammohanarasā chādanapaccupaṭṭhānā; abhisaṅkharaṇalakkhaṇā saṅkhārā āyūhanarasā; vijānanalakkhaṇaṃ viññāṇaṃ pubbaṅgamarasaṃ. "Kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassā"ti ca dukkhasamūhasseva samudayo vutto, na sattassa, na sukhasubhādīnaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "satto ayaṃ, mama eso attabhāvo"ti sattasaññaṃ pahāya, avijjādīnaṃ lakkhaṇarasapaccupaṭṭhānapadaṭṭhānavasena sallakkhaṇaṃ upasampādetvā, "suddho dukkhakkhandho samudeti, suddho nirujjhatī"ti passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Paṭicca)

[[#^toc-6-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye ekacittakkhaṇiko paccayākāro dassito. #### Paccayacatukkaṃ (Uddesa)

[[#^toc-6-2-1|↑↑↑]]

Paccayacatukke cattāro vārā — paṭhamakā dve arūpabhave, tatiyo rūpabhave, catuttho kāmabhave paccayākāradassanatthaṃ vuttā. Saṅkhārassa hi avijjā sahajātaaññamaññanissayasampayuttaatthiavigatapaccayehi chahi hetupaccayena cāti sattadhā paccayo; viññāṇassa saṅkhāro aṭṭhadhā, nāmassa viññāṇaṃ navadhāti evaṃ anekadhā paccayabhāvo veditabbo. "Yaṃ yattha aññathā vuttaṃ, avuttañcāpi yaṃ yahiṃ; yaṃ yathā paccayo yassa, taṃ sabbamupalakkhaye"ti. Tasmā yoginā "ekova paccayo ekassa phalassā"ti ekaṃsagāhaṃ pahāya, anekapaccayasamodhānato phaluppattidassanaṃ upasampādetvā, attano cittakkhaṇe bahūnaṃ paccayānaṃ samavāyaṃ passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Hetucatukkaṃ (Uddesa)

[[#^toc-6-2-2|↑↑↑]]

Hetucatukke "avijjāhetuko"ti avigatapaccayatā dassitā — avijjā saṅkhārassa sahavattanato yāva bhaṅgā pavattikā gamikā. #### Aññamaññacatukkaṃ (Uddesa)

#### Akusalaniddeso

[[#^toc-6-2-6|↑↑↑]]

Akusalaniddese akusalacittesu paccayākāro vibhatto. Ekacittakkhaṇe taṇhāya ca kāmupādānassa ca asambhavā diṭṭhivasena upādānaṃ dassitaṃ; diṭṭhivippayuttesu upādānaṭṭhāne adhimokkhena, domanassasahagatesu taṇhāṭṭhāne paṭighena, vicikicchāsampayutte vicikicchāya, uddhaccasampayutte uddhaccena padaṃ pūritaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "akasmā me idaṃ akusalacittaṃ uppannaṃ, ahetuka"nti sammohaṃ pahāya, ekamekasmiṃ akusalacitte avijjāsaṅkhārādīnaṃ paṭipāṭidassanaṃ upasampādetvā, "sahetukaṃ idaṃ paccayasamuppannaṃ, paccaye chinne nirujjhatī"ti passanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

#### Avijjāmūlakakusalaniddeso

#### Kusalamūlakavipākaniddeso

## Satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-7|↑↑↑]]

Satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅge cattāro satipaṭṭhānā anūnā anadhikā vuttā subhasukhaniccaattavipallāsānaṃ pahānatthaṃ, veneyyapuggalānañca caritānurūpato visuddhimaggadassanatthaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "aññena maggena aññena vā ārammaṇena nibbānaṃ pāpuṇissāmī"ti anekaggāhaṃ pahāya, attano caritaṃ ñatvā yaṃ oḷārikaṃ vā sukhumaṃ vā ārammaṇaṃ sappāyaṃ, tattheva satiṃ upaṭṭhapetvā, "catudvāre nagare catūhi disāhi āgacchantā ekaṃyeva nagaraṃ pavisanti, evaṃ cattāropi satipaṭṭhānā ekaṃyeva nibbānaṃ osarantī"ti sallakkhento, ekasmimpi ārammaṇe alīnena vīriyena yuñjitvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Satipaṭṭhāna)

[[#^toc-7-1|↑↑↑]]

Uddesavāre "ātāpī sampajāno satimā vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassa"nti sampayogaṅgañca pahānaṅgañca dassitaṃ; anātāpino hi antosaṅkhepo antarāyakaro, asampajāno upāyapariggahe sammuyhati, muṭṭhassati ca asamattho hoti. Tasmā yoginā abhijjhāgahaṇena saṅgahitaṃ kāmacchandaṃ domanassaggahaṇena ca saṅgahitaṃ byāpādaṃ tadaṅgavinayena vikkhambhanavinayena vā vinayitvā, kilesānaṃ ātāpanasamatthaṃ vīriyaṃ pariggāhakañca ñāṇaṃ pariggāhikañca satiṃ upasampādetvā, "na ettha kāyavinimutto satto vā itthī vā puriso vā koci dhammo atthi, rathasambhārasamūho viya nagarāvayavasamūho viya ca ayaṃ kesalomādisamūhamatto"ti asammissato vavatthapento ghanavinibbhogaṃ katvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Satipaṭṭhāna)

[[#^toc-7-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye lokuttarasatipaṭṭhānavasena desanā āraddhā; tattha kāyādiārammaṇesu tanti aṭṭhapetvā suddhikapaṭipadāsuññatāppaṇihitādibhedena asītinayasahassāni dassitāni. Tasmā yoginā "lokiyamattameva satipaṭṭhānaṃ, ito uttari natthī"ti omānaṃ pahāya, "yaṃ pubbabhāge nānācittehi kāyavedanācittadhamme pariggaṇhāti, tadeva vipassanaṃ ussukkāpetvā maggakkhaṇe ekāya maggasampayuttāya satiyā catukiccasādhanavasena catunāmaṃ hotī"ti adhigamaparāyaṇaṃ dassanaṃ upasampādetvā, nibbānārammaṇaṃ lokuttarasatipaṭṭhānaṃ patthayamānena appamattena vipassanaṃ vaḍḍhetvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Satipaṭṭhāna)

[[#^toc-7-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake nibbattitalokuttarāneva satipaṭṭhānāni kathitāni; tāni appamāṇaṃ nibbānaṃ ārabbha pavattanato appamāṇārammaṇāni, sahajātahetuvasena maggahetukāni, vīriyavīmaṃsajeṭṭhakakāle maggādhipatīni, nibbānassa bahiddhādhammattā bahiddhārammaṇāni. Tasmā yoginā "satipaṭṭhānaṃ maggameva ārammaṇaṃ karotī"ti vā "attano cittameva parittaṃ ārammaṇaṃ hotī"ti vā micchāgāhaṃ pahāya, appamāṇārammaṇataṃ bahiddhārammaṇatañca yathāsabhāvato sallakkhaṇaṃ upasampādetvā, "suttantabhājanīye lokiyalokuttaramissakā, idha pana lokuttarāyevā"ti tiparivaṭṭaṃ desanaṃ anugacchanto, appamāṇe nibbāne ninnapoṇapabbhāracitto hutvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## Sammappadhānavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-8|↑↑↑]]

Sammappadhānavibhaṅge cattāro sammappadhānā kāraṇappadhānā upāyappadhānā yonisopadhānāti vuttā – anuppannānaṃ pāpakānaṃ anuppādāya, uppannānaṃ pahānāya, anuppannānaṃ kusalānaṃ uppādāya, uppannānaṃ ṭhitiyā. Tasmā yoginā "sabhāveneva kilesā na uppajjissanti, kusalañca sayameva vaḍḍhissatī"ti nirussāhabhāvaṃ pahāya, catūsupi ṭhānesu chandaṃ janetvā vāyamitvā vīriyaṃ ārabhitvā cittaṃ paggahetvā padahanaṃ upasampādetvā, "ekameva vīriyaṃ maggakkhaṇe catukiccasādhanavasena cattāri nāmāni labhatī"ti sallakkhento, ekasmiṃyeva vīriye catubbidhaṃ kiccaṃ sādhento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Sammappadhāna)

[[#^toc-8-1|↑↑↑]]

Uddesaniddesavāresu chandavīriyacittapaggahapadhānāni bhājetvā dassitāni; tattha "chando"ti na taṇhāchando na diṭṭhichando, atha kho kattukamyatākusaladhammacchando adhippeto. Vinicchaye ca dassitaṃ – vattaganthadhutaṅgasamāpattivipassanānavakammesu aññatarena yuttassa kilesā okāsaṃ na labhanti, tesu pana vissaṭṭhesu ayonisomanasikārañceva sativossaggañca āgamma uppajjantiyeva. Tasmā yoginā "kilesā me vūpasantā, kataṃ karaṇīya"nti asamudācāramattena vissāsaṃ pahāya, yasmiṃ vatte vā ganthe vā dhutaṅge vā samāpattiyaṃ vā vipassanāyaṃ vā ṭhitassa kilesā okāsaṃ na labhanti, tadeva avissajjento sātaccakiriyaṃ upasampādetvā, "vikkhambhitamattā ete, na samugghātitā; asamugghātitā hi bhūmiladdhuppannā nāma maggavajjhā"ti sallakkhento, ananubhūtārammaṇesupi ayonisomanasikāraṃ paṭibāhanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Sammappadhāna)

[[#^toc-8-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye sammappadhānāni nibbattitalokuttarāneva vīsatinayasahassavasena vibhattāni; **vipāke pana sammappadhānehi kattabbakiccaṃ natthīti vipākavāro na gahito**. Tasmā yoginā "puññaphalena sayameva padhānaṃ nipphajjissatī"ti vipākapaccāsīsanaṃ pahāya, "padhānaṃ nāma kattabbameva, na paṭilabhitabbaṃ; kusalapakkheyeva tassa okāso"ti niṭṭhaṃ upasampādetvā, appamatto hutvā attanā kātabbaṃ kiccaṃ anaññassa nikkhipanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Sammappadhāna)

[[#^toc-8-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake sammappadhānā appamāṇārammaṇā maggahetukā ca vuttā; vīmaṃsajeṭṭhikāya maggabhāvanāya maggādhipatīni, **vīriyajeṭṭhikāya pana aññassa vīriyassa abhāvā na vattabbāneva** – sammappadhānaṃ hi sayameva vīriyaṃ. Tasmā yoginā "aññaṃ kiñci balaṃ nissāya padahissāmī"ti parāyattagāhaṃ pahāya, "yaṃ padahati tadeva vīriyaṃ, natthi tato añño padhānakārako"ti attapaccakkhaṃ dassanaṃ upasampādetvā, appamāṇe nibbāne ārammaṇaṃ ṭhapento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## Iddhipādavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-9|↑↑↑]]

Iddhipādavibhaṅge ijjhanakaṭṭhena iddhi, patiṭṭhānaṭṭhena pādoti cattāro iddhipādā vuttā. ### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Iddhipāda)

[[#^toc-9-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake iddhipādā appamāṇārammaṇā maggahetukā, **na pana maggādhipatino – cattāro hi adhipatayo aññamaññaṃ garuṃ na karonti sayaṃ jeṭṭhakattā**, samajātikā cattāro rājaputtā viya. Tasmā yoginā "sabbepi cattāro ekato jeṭṭhake katvā bhāvessāmī"ti asambhavagāhaṃ pahāya, ekasmiṃ adhipatimhi ṭhitassa itaresaṃ sahajātabhāvadassanaṃ upasampādetvā, appamāṇe bahiddhārammaṇe nibbāne ninnacitto hutvā cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

## Bojjhaṅgavibhaṅgo

[[#^toc-10|↑↑↑]]

Bojjhaṅgavibhaṅge satta bojjhaṅgā anūnā anadhikā vuttā līnuddhaccapaṭipakkhato sabbatthikato ca – dhammavicayavīriyapītiyo līnassa paṭipakkhā, passaddhisamādhiupekkhā uddhaccassa, sati pana loṇadhūpanaṃ viya sabbabyañjanesu sabbattha icchitabbā. Tasmā yoginā "sabbadā sabbe samaṃ bhāvetabbā"ti aviciṇṇagāhaṃ pahāya, "yasmiṃ samaye līnaṃ cittaṃ, tasmiṃ dhammavicayavīriyapītīnaṃ kālo; yasmiṃ uddhataṃ, tasmiṃ passaddhisamādhiupekkhānaṃ kālo"ti kālaññutaṃ upasampādetvā, sabbattha pana satiṃ anapanento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Suttantabhājanīyaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-1|↑↑↑]]

Paṭhamanaye satisambojjhaṅganiddese paññā saṅgahitā satiyā balavabhāvadīpanatthaṃ – rājaputtaṃ gahetvā ṭhito mahāmatto viya paññāya saddhiṃ uppannā sati balavatī, attano dhammatāya ṭhito viya vinā paññāya uppannā dubbalā. Tatiyanaye ca vivekanissitaṃ virāganissitaṃ nirodhanissitaṃ vossaggapariṇāmiñca bhāveti, vossaggo ca pariccāgavossaggo pakkhandanavossaggoti duvidho. Tasmā yoginā "satimattena kataṃ karaṇīya"nti dubbalasatiyā santuṭṭhiṃ pahāya, cirakataṃ cirabhāsitañca saritvā tameva dhammaṃ paññāya pavicinanto anussaraṇena saddhiṃ pavicayaṃ upasampādetvā, tadaṅgavasena kilese pariccajanto tanninnabhāvena ca nibbānaṃ pakkhandanto – vipassanākkhaṇe kiccato tadaṅgavivekaṃ, ajjhāsayato nissaraṇavivekaṃ nissāya – cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Abhidhammabhājanīyaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-2|↑↑↑]]

Abhidhammabhājanīye upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo "upekkhitabbayutte samappavatte dhamme ikkhati na codetī"ti vutto, majjhattatā ca cittassa, na sattassāti dassitā; phalakkhaṇepi ca bojjhaṅgā labbhantīti vipākanayopi āraddho. Tasmā yoginā "ahaṃ majjhatto, mama upekkhā"ti attagāhaṃ pahāya, samappavattesu dhammesu acodanaṃ abyāvaṭataṃ upasampādetvā, "cittasseva ayaṃ majjhattatā, na puggalassā"ti sallakkhento, paggahaniggahakāle katakicco sārathi viya samappavattesu assesu ajjhupekkhanto cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.

### Pañhāpucchakaṃ (Bojjhaṅga)

[[#^toc-10-3|↑↑↑]]

Pañhāpucchake bojjhaṅgā appamāṇaṃ nibbānaṃ ārabbha pavattanato appamāṇārammaṇā, bahiddhārammaṇā ca vuttā; suttantabhājanīyassa paṭhamanaye lokiyā, dutiyatatiyesu missakā, abhidhammabhājanīyapañhāpucchakesu pana lokuttarāyeva. Tasmā yoginā "ye me idāni uppajjanti bojjhaṅgā, teyeva lokuttarā"ti abhimānaṃ pahāya, lokiyato missakato lokuttarato ca anukkamena bhāvanaṃ upasampādetvā, "appamāṇaṃ ārammaṇaṃ appamāṇova bojjhaṅgo karotī"ti sallakkhento, parittesu ārammaṇesu cittaṃ asaṇṭhapento cittaṃ pariyodāpetabbaṃ.
