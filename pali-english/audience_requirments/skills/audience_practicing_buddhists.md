# Audience Profile: Practicing Buddhists

## Audience

**Practitioners** who study and apply the teachings — meditators, dharma students, retreatants, committed lay Buddhists.

**Assume they:**

* know common Buddhist English (mindfulness, craving, clinging, hindrances, path)
* may know some Pāli from practice contexts (jhāna, kamma, saṅkhāra, vipassanā)
* want language that matches **how teachings are discussed in practice communities**

**Do not assume they:** are academic Pāli scholars or need full Abhidhamma precision on every lemma.

---

## Translation Goal

Preserve **doctrinal meaning** and **practice-relevant distinctions** in language practitioners recognize and can use in reflection and discussion.

---

## Terminology

Use language practitioners recognize from study and practice. For each doctrinal sense, pick **one** rendering and reuse it **every time** that sense appears.

### Word-choice rules (practicing Buddhists)

| Rule | Do | Don't |
| ---- | -- | ----- |
| Practice terms | Retain widely used terms: **Dhamma**, **Nibbāna**, **Saṅgha**, **jhāna**, **kamma**, **vipassanā**, **mindfulness**, **clinging**, **hindrance** | Over-simplify into non-Buddhist psychology language |
| Abhidhamma | Standard practice-community English ("aggregate", "formation", "consciousness") | Obscure philological labels without need |
| Loanwords | Use Pāli where practitioners expect it; English where that is the norm in their tradition | Inconsistent mixing in the same sense |
| Distinctions | Keep Sense Tags separate (e.g. citta vs viññāṇa) | Merging for "readability" at the cost of practice meaning |

### Examples (lemma, sense → rendering)

* citta, `mind_heart` → mind
* vedanā, `pleasant_unpleasant` → feeling
* saṅkhāra, `mental_formation` → formations
* viññāṇa, `awareness_consciousness` → consciousness
* sati, `mindfulness_presence` → mindfulness
* upādāna, `grasping_clinging` → clinging
* nīvaraṇa, `obstacle_obstruction_barrier` → hindrance
* vipassanā, `insight_penetrating` → insight

---

## Style

* **Clear**, **natural**, **respectful**
* **Practice-oriented** — language that supports contemplation, not display of terminology

---

## Explanatory Additions

Light clarification through wording choices is permitted.

Do not insert commentary.

---

## Priority

1. **Doctrinal accuracy** for practice
2. **Practical comprehension**
3. **Consistency**
4. **Readability**
